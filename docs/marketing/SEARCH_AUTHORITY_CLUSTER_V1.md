## Official source base

Regulatory content must be checked against current official EU material before publication:

- European Commission Article 50 guidance and FAQs
- AI Act Service Desk Articles 3 and 50
- AI Act Service Desk current enforcement/timeline FAQs
- EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 consolidated/current text

Current verified campaign facts:

- Article 50 transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026.
- A limited transition until 2 December 2026 applies to the Article 50(2) marking/detection duty for certain systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026.
- Provider and deployer are distinct Article 3 roles; role analysis should be system-specific and fact-specific.
- Current official implementation material places Annex III high-risk rules at 2 December 2027 and Annex I regulated-product high-risk rules at 2 August 2028.

Public content must not hide exceptions, claim universal obligations or provide legal advice.

## 1. Mission

Build credible distribution, learning and future channel relationships inside European AI ecosystems without pretending that memberships, partnerships or endorsements already exist.

Operating model:

`ECOSYSTEM -> CONTRIBUTE -> EARN TRUST -> CO-CREATE -> DISTRIBUTE -> REFER / PARTNER`

Public signal:

- describes itself as Germany's largest AI network;
- 700+ members shown on current public site;
- connects AI/deep-tech companies with established business and politics;
- provides member platform, marketplace, events and policy work;
- has categories including Legal & Compliance and testing/certification of AI products.

Sources:

- https://ki-verband.de/
- https://ki-verband.de/community/ki-unternehmen/

RISCK COMPLY could contribute practical material on operational AI governance, AI inventory, Article 50 implementation or evidence workflows.

First move:

- identify open membership/working-group route through official channels;
- offer a practical non-sales educational contribution only after establishing fit.

## 4. Consultancy partner profile

Prioritize consultancies that:

- implement AI for European mid-market/enterprise clients;
- already encounter AI Act/security/procurement questions;
- do not sell a directly competing governance platform as core product;
- manage several clients and would benefit from repeatable governance workflows.

Potential value proposition:

> Use RISCK COMPLY as the structured operating workspace behind AI-governance engagements — systems, owners, risk/review context, evidence, tasks and history — while the consultancy retains advisory responsibility.

Never claim that RISCK COMPLY replaces the consultancy or legal analysis.

## 5. Partner qualification

Score 0–100:

- ICP overlap: 0–25
- distribution/member reach: 0–20
- governance/regulatory relevance: 0–20
- ability to co-create useful content: 0–15
- commercial/referral potential: 0–10
- reputation/trust fit: 0–10

Priority bands:

- 80+: strategic relationship
- 65–79: active nurture
- 50–64: monitor/engage
- <50: no active effort

## 6. Contribution-first sequence

### Step 1 — Learn

Read current public work/events/guidance.

### Step 2 — Engage

Comment/share only when RISCK COMPLY can add a precise useful perspective.

### Step 3 — Offer an asset

Examples:

- AI System Inventory Template;
- Article 50 decision map;
- provider/deployer operational record;
- evidence checklist.

### Step 4 — Co-create

Only after mutual interest:

- webinar;
- article;
- working-group input;
- workshop;
- research snapshot.

### Step 5 — Partner commercially

Only where there is a clear customer/referral/use-case fit.

Do not jump directly from LinkedIn follow to `partner announcement`.

## 7. Partner-safe claims

Allowed:

- `RISCK COMPLY is exploring ways to contribute to...` only when true.
- `member` only after actual membership.
- `partner` only after explicit relationship.
- logos only with permission and applicable brand rules.

Never write:

- `approved by`;
- `endorsed by`;
- `recommended by`;
- `official AI Act partner`;

unless explicit evidence supports the exact claim.

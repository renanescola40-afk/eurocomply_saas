# 1. HUB FRANCE IA — PRIORITY A+

## Why this relationship is relevant

Hub France IA is active in responsible/sovereign AI ecosystem work and has published practical risk-management material for AI systems. Its community and project structure make practical governance education a natural contribution route.

## Contribution proposal A

### Working title

**De l’inventaire IA au système de gouvernance opérationnelle : 7 champs pour rendre les obligations traçables**

### Format

20–30 minute practical webinar or short member article.

### Audience

PME/ETI, AI providers, compliance/legal teams, AI programme owners.

### Learning outcomes

Participants should leave able to structure one operational record around:

1. système / cas d’usage;
2. responsable;
3. fournisseur / dépendances;
4. rôle réglementaire à analyser;
5. exigence / contrôle;
6. preuve;
7. date ou trigger de revue.

### Boundaries

- not legal advice;
- no automatic AI Act classification;
- no claim that one schema satisfies all obligations;
- use current official EU sources for legal timing.

## Contribution proposal B

### Working title

**Article 50 après le 2 août 2026 : transformer une question réglementaire en workflow opérationnel**

### Structure

- Article 50 is not one universal disclosure rule;
- start from system/use case and organisational role;
- identify the relevant transparency question;
- assign owner;
- capture implementation evidence;
- create review trigger/history.

### Useful downloadable companion

`Article 50 Operational Decision Map — FR`

## Initial relationship note — French

Bonjour,

je suis avec intérêt les travaux du Hub France IA sur la gestion des risques, l’IA responsable et l’adoption opérationnelle par les organisations.

Chez RISCK COMPLY, nous travaillons sur une question très pratique : comment transformer les sujets de gouvernance IA — inventaire, responsabilités, rôle, preuves et revues — en un système opérable par les équipes, sans prétendre remplacer l’analyse juridique.

Nous avons préparé deux ressources très concrètes : un modèle d’inventaire IA opérationnel et une carte Article 50 orientée workflow. Si cela peut être utile à un groupe de travail, un article membre ou un format pédagogique du Hub, je serais heureux de les partager pour avis avant toute discussion de collaboration.

# 2. KI BUNDESVERBAND — PRIORITY A

## Why this relationship is relevant

KI Bundesverband is a major German AI-industry network and publicly engages on AI Act implementation. Its 2026 policy material calls for practical templates and coherent compliance pathways, which aligns with a contribution focused on operating records rather than legal marketing.

## Contribution proposal A

### Working title

**Vom KI-Inventar zum operativen Governance-System: Wie Unternehmen Verantwortlichkeit, Nachweise und Reviews verbinden**

### Format

Practical member article, webinar or workshop contribution.

### Core model

`KI-SYSTEM -> USE CASE -> VERANTWORTLICHER -> ROLLE/KONTEXT -> NACHWEIS -> REVIEW`

### Audience

German AI companies, Mittelstand adopters, Legal/Compliance, Product, Security.

## Contribution proposal B

### Working title

**Artikel 50 ohne Label-Mythos: Ein operativer Entscheidungsrahmen für Unternehmen**

### Core teaching points

- Article 50 is not `label all AI`;
- separate provider and deployer duties;
- assess per system/use case;
- map implementation to evidence;
- review after meaningful change.

# 3. ADIGITAL — PRIORITY A

## Why this relationship is relevant

Adigital has long engaged on AI regulation, regulatory sandboxes and public-private implementation approaches in Spain. The strongest contribution is therefore not generic AI Act education, but a practical layer translating regulation into repeatable internal operating processes.

## Contribution proposal A

### Working title

**Del Reglamento de IA al proceso operativo: cómo estructurar inventario, responsables, evidencia y revisión**

### Format

Short practical article, member session or roundtable contribution.

### Audience

Spanish digital businesses, AI providers, adopters, compliance/legal teams.

### Core framework

`SISTEMA -> CASO DE USO -> ROL -> RESPONSABLE -> REQUISITO -> EVIDENCIA -> REVISIÓN`

## Contribution proposal B

### Working title

**Qué aprendimos del sandbox: la gobernanza de IA necesita registros operativos, no solo checklists**

### Angle

Use sandbox/regulatory-learning context as the starting point, then show the internal operating layer that companies need to maintain after an assessment or pilot:

- inventory;
- owner;
- rationale;
- evidence;
- actions;
- change/review triggers.

Do not imply that RISCK COMPLY participated in Spain's sandbox unless that ever becomes true.

# 4. Reusable contribution asset — Operational AI Governance Canvas

## One-page structure

### Column 1 — System facts

- system name;
- use case;
- business process;
- countries/regions;
- model/provider dependencies.

### Column 2 — Accountability

- business owner;
- technical owner;
- legal/compliance reviewer;
- security/privacy reviewer where relevant.

### Column 3 — Governance context

- provider/deployer context to analyse;
- risk/review status;
- relevant policy/control;
- transparency question where relevant.

### Column 4 — Evidence

- decision rationale;
- implementation proof;
- vendor/security evidence;
- customer/procurement evidence.

### Column 5 — Continuous review

- last review;
- next review;
- change trigger;
- owner of follow-up;
- activity/history link.

## Safe footer

`This canvas supports operational AI-governance work. It does not provide legal classification, certification or a compliance guarantee.`

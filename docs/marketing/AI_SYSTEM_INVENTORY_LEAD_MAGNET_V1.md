## Instructions section

The resource should explain:

1. Start with facts, not legal conclusions.
2. Assign an accountable owner for every system/use case.
3. Keep provider/vendor and organizational role separate.
4. Record why a review/classification conclusion was reached.
5. Link evidence to the record instead of storing context only in inboxes/folders.
6. Define a next-review trigger/date.
7. Revisit the record after material system, provider, use-case or deployment changes.

## What the template should NOT claim

Do not say:

- completing the template makes the organization compliant;
- every field is legally mandatory for every system;
- the template produces a binding EU AI Act classification;
- RISCK COMPLY replaces legal counsel;
- every AI system is high-risk or subject to the same obligations.

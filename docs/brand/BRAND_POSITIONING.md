# RISCK COMPLY Brand Positioning

Date: 2026-07-06

## Primary brand

RISCK COMPLY

Customer-facing surfaces should use RISCK COMPLY as the visible brand. EuroComply may remain only as legacy repository or internal historical naming where removing it would be risky.

## Category

AI governance readiness and compliance operations platform.

## Audience

European B2B teams that use AI and need a structured way to organize governance work: founders, CTOs, compliance officers, security teams, operations leaders, procurement-facing SaaS teams, AI agencies and consultancies.

## Core pain

Companies are adopting AI faster than their documentation, ownership and risk workflows can keep up.

## Promise

RISCK COMPLY helps teams turn scattered AI usage into organized AI governance operations.

## Safe value pillars

1. AI inventory: systems, owners, providers, use cases, countries and data context.
2. Risk visibility: structured signals, review status, ownership and next actions.
3. Evidence preparation: reviewable documents, summaries and governance records connected to the inventory.
4. Team workflows: owners, stakeholders, tasks and activity history.
5. Trust documentation: security, privacy, DPA, subprocessors and disclosure pages written conservatively.

## Tone of voice

Clear, serious, confident, conservative on assurance claims, direct about limitations and strong on operational value.

## Tagline options

Primary: AI governance readiness for European B2B teams.

Secondary: Organize AI inventory, risk visibility and evidence preparation in one workspace.

Enterprise: Advanced governance support for teams preparing AI evidence at scale.

## Messaging rules

Use language around readiness, support, workflows, evidence preparation, risk visibility, trust documentation, security review support and procurement support.

Avoid language that suggests the product alone creates legal outcomes, unsupported assurance, advisor replacement or automatic buyer acceptance.

## Homepage headline direction

Recommended: Prepare AI Act readiness without unsafe legal promises.

Alternative: Turn scattered AI usage into reviewable governance evidence.

## Footer positioning

Recommended: AI Act readiness support, governance evidence preparation and risk visibility for modern European teams.

## Product naming

Visible plan names: Starter, Growth and Enterprise.

Visible modules: AI Inventory, Risk Visibility, Evidence Preparation, Governance Workflows, Trust Center and Audit Trail.

## Brand cleanup rule

When customer-facing copy references EuroComply, replace it with RISCK COMPLY unless the file is historical documentation, migration evidence, repository metadata or a compatibility note.

## 1. Controller identity

The controller for website, account, commercial, billing, support and security processing is **[FOUNDER FACT REQUIRED: legal entity, address, registration and contact details]**. DPO or EU representative details, if applicable, are **[FOUNDER FACT REQUIRED]**.

When customers upload personal data to their workspace, the customer generally acts as controller and the provider acts as processor under the DPA, subject to counsel-confirmed role allocation.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers public websites, account registration, authentication, service administration, billing, support, security, product communications and optional analytics. Customer-controlled workspace processing is additionally governed by the DPA.

## 3. Data categories

Depending on configuration, the service may process:

- account identifiers, email and authentication/session metadata;
- organisation, membership, role and permission records;
- customer-entered AI systems, vendors, risks, documents, tasks, assessments and evidence;
- audit, security, authorisation-denial and step-up metadata;
- billing identifiers, subscriptions and payment metadata received from the payment provider;
- support requests and communications;
- device, diagnostic, log, rate-limit and abuse-prevention metadata;
- optional product analytics and consent preferences.

The production data inventory and special-category-data posture require founder confirmation.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

Processing purposes include providing and administering the service, authenticating users, securing tenants, billing, support, compliance with legal obligations, fraud/abuse prevention, incident response and product improvement where enabled.

## 5. Sources

Data may be provided by users, organisation administrators, authorised customer integrations, payment providers, authentication providers and operational systems. The service should not infer sensitive characteristics for unrelated purposes.

## 6. Sharing and subprocessors

Data is shared only as necessary with configured infrastructure, authentication/database, payment, observability, analytics, email, support and professional-service providers, or where required by law. The active subprocessor register must match production configuration and the signed DPA.

Production regions, transfer locations and mechanisms are **[FOUNDER FACT REQUIRED]**. Counsel must confirm adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses, supplementary measures and customer notice language where applicable.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Retention periods for accounts, customer content, billing, support, logs, backups and audit records are **[FOUNDER FACT REQUIRED]** and must align with `docs/privacy/DATA_PROCESSING_OVERVIEW.md`, the retention policy and provider capabilities.

Deletion requests are controlled review operations. Some billing, fraud, legal-hold, backup and audit-chain records may be retained where lawful and necessary, with minimisation and access restrictions.

## 9. Security

The service uses authentication, organisation scoping, role checks, RLS posture, step-up controls, audit events, no-store responses and managed cloud providers where configured. Security measures are evidence-bound; no certification, audit, penetration-test, backup or recovery claim applies unless specifically documented.

## 10. Rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Customer workspace requests may need to be directed to the customer as controller. Contact channels, verification, response ownership and statutory periods require founder and counsel confirmation.

## 11. Cookies and analytics

Essential storage may be used for authentication, security, preferences and service operation. Optional analytics or marketing technologies must be enabled only with the required consent and configuration. A jurisdiction-appropriate cookie notice and consent record are **[COUNSEL DECISION REQUIRED]**.

## 12. Automated decisions

Risck Comply outputs support human compliance operations. The provider does not intend website/account processing to make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about individuals. Customer use of product outputs remains the customer’s responsibility and may require separate assessment.

## 13. Children

The business service is not directed to children. Age threshold, prohibited customer uses and handling of inadvertently submitted children’s data require counsel confirmation.

## 14. Complaints

Individuals may contact **[FOUNDER FACT REQUIRED: privacy contact]** and may complain to the competent supervisory authority. The lead authority and cross-border establishment position require counsel confirmation.

Material changes should be dated and communicated appropriately. The publication date and previous-version archive must be maintained.

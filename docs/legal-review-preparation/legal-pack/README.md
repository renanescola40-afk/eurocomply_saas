# Contract, Privacy and Counsel Handoff Pack

All documents in this directory are **review drafts**, not final legal instruments. They are designed to reduce counsel drafting time by separating:

- repository-proven technical facts;
- founder facts that require signed confirmation;
- legal choices requiring qualified counsel;
- customer-specific or external evidence.

## Status rules

- `REVIEW_DRAFT`: prepared but not legally approved.
- `FOUNDER_FACT_REQUIRED`: cannot be completed from repository evidence.
- `COUNSEL_DECISION_REQUIRED`: requires a qualified legal choice.
- `CUSTOMER_SPECIFIC`: must be handled in an order form, customer DPA or separate advice.
- `ACCEPTED`: prohibited unless the exact-SHA legal evidence gate validates a signed decision.

## Precedence

A signed customer agreement and approved DPA prevail over public summaries. Public trust pages must not create broader commitments than signed documents or available evidence.

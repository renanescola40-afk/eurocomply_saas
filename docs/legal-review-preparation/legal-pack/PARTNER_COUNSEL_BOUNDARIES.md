# Partner Counsel Boundaries — Review Draft

**Status:** `REVIEW_DRAFT` · `FOUNDER_FACT_REQUIRED` · `COUNSEL_DECISION_REQUIRED`

## 1. Separate services

Risck Comply provides software, workflow, evidence and document-preparation support. A partner lawyer or law firm provides independent legal services under a separate engagement. Use of the platform alone does not create a lawyer-client relationship.

## 2. Engagement and fees

The customer must receive clear disclosure of:

- the lawyer/law firm identity and jurisdiction;
- scope, exclusions and deliverables;
- conflicts process and professional rules;
- fees, billing and payment recipient;
- complaint and termination rights;
- whether Risck Comply receives any referral fee or commercial benefit.

The referral, marketplace, revenue-share and payment model is **[FOUNDER FACT AND COUNSEL DECISION REQUIRED]**.

## 3. Independence and no control

Counsel must exercise independent professional judgment. Risck Comply must not direct legal conclusions, require approval to change a finding or condition payment on a favourable opinion.

Product status may display `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `CHANGES_REQUIRED` or `COUNSEL_ACCEPTED` only from an authenticated and verifiable decision record.

## 4. Conflicts and qualification

Counsel is responsible for conflict checks, client identification, jurisdictional qualification and engagement acceptance. The platform may collect structured declarations but cannot validate professional standing without external verification.

## 5. Confidentiality and privilege

Customers must be told that:

- privilege depends on jurisdiction, purpose, participants and handling;
- storing material in the SaaS does not automatically create or preserve privilege;
- legal-review workspaces should use least privilege and controlled sharing;
- confidential signed opinions should be stored in the approved confidential evidence location, not a public repository;
- disclosure to providers or third parties may affect confidentiality and must follow the DPA and engagement terms.

## 6. Data roles

The customer and counsel must determine controller/processor roles for legal-review data. Risck Comply processes workspace data under the customer agreement and DPA; counsel may be an independent controller or processor depending on the engagement and jurisdiction.

## 7. Product outputs

AI-generated summaries, checklists and draft documents are preparation materials. Counsel must review source materials and exercise independent judgment rather than relying solely on generated outputs.

## 8. Decision artifact

A creditable legal decision must include reviewer identity, professional registration, jurisdiction, qualification scope, conflicts/independence declarations, exact product SHA, evidence digest, findings, conditions, validity, signature reference, decision digest and timestamp.

## 9. Liability and complaints

Risck Comply’s software liability and counsel’s professional liability must remain separate. Customer-facing documents must identify the correct complaint route and must not imply that Risck Comply provides professional indemnity coverage for counsel.

## 10. Prohibited representations

Do not state that:

- partner counsel certifies the entire product unless the signed scope says so;
- a review guarantees customer compliance;
- the platform’s score is a legal opinion;
- counsel is an employee or agent unless factually and legally true;
- privilege is guaranteed.

## Approval gate

Before launching a referral or embedded-counsel model, obtain professional-regulation advice for the target jurisdictions and approve commercial terms, data sharing, fee arrangements, conflicts, privilege language and liability allocation.

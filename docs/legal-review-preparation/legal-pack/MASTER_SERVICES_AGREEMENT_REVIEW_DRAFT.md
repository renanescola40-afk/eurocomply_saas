It must not be signed or represented as effective until authoritative party facts, applicable tax/company facts, product/service facts, DPA and security commitments have been reconciled and an authorised representative approves the exact version.

## 1. Parties and order structure

The intended supplier is **SAMUEL CERQUEIRA, UNIPESSOAL LDA**, subject to final authoritative confirmation of registered office, registration/tax identifiers and signatory authority.

The customer is the legal entity identified in the applicable Order Form.

The intended contract stack is:
1. signed Order Form;
2. signed or validly incorporated DPA for applicable processing;
3. expressly negotiated schedules/addenda;
4. this MSA;
5. incorporated service/support/security documents only to the extent expressly stated.

Conflicts and precedence require qualified legal confirmation before execution.

## 2. Services

RISCK COMPLY provides B2B AI governance and compliance-operations software. Exact subscribed capabilities, seats, limits, add-ons, support tier, service period and commercial terms are defined in the Order Form and the billing authority applicable to that customer.

RISCK COMPLY supports governance workflows and evidence preparation. It does not provide a certification, regulator decision, legal opinion or guarantee that a customer is compliant with the EU AI Act, GDPR or any other law.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

The customer is responsible for:
- authorised and lawful use of the service;
- accuracy and lawfulness of Customer Content and instructions;
- assigning appropriate users and access rights;
- obtaining any consents, notices or lawful bases required for data the customer provides;
- human review of compliance outputs and legal conclusions;
- complying with applicable use restrictions and the agreed acceptable-use terms.

## 4. Fees, taxes and billing

Fees, billing interval, currency, taxes, renewal mechanics, invoicing details, purchase-order references and any negotiated credits or discounts are stated in the Order Form or applicable billing record.

No final tax/VAT treatment is incorporated by this draft until the supplier's authoritative Portuguese tax facts and invoice requirements are confirmed.

## 5. Term, renewal and cancellation

The service term and any renewal model are defined in the Order Form.

The repository owner-selected baseline currently contemplates auto-renewal for the same billing period until cancelled, subject to agreed notice, mandatory law and the exact customer order. Final enforceability and notice mechanics require legal review.

## 6. Suspension

RISCK COMPLY may suspend affected access where reasonably necessary for security, unlawful use, fraud, material platform risk, provider restriction or material breach, subject to the final agreed notice/cure mechanics and mandatory law.

Ordinary non-payment cure and restoration mechanics must remain aligned with the implemented billing lifecycle.

## 7. Data protection

Where RISCK COMPLY processes Customer Personal Data as processor/subprocessor, the applicable DPA governs that processing.

Controller/processor roles are activity-specific. No clause in this MSA should override mandatory GDPR requirements or create unsupported transfer, deletion, residency or security guarantees.

## 8. Security and confidentiality

Each party will protect the other's confidential information using reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and agreed risk.

RISCK COMPLY security commitments must remain limited to attributable controls and evidence. No SOC 2, ISO 27001, clean pentest, fixed RPO/RTO, availability percentage or other assurance is incorporated unless expressly evidenced and stated in the Order Form or an agreed schedule.

## 9. Intellectual property

Subject to final title-chain confirmation:
- the customer retains rights in Customer Content;
- RISCK COMPLY and/or its lawful licensors retain rights in the service, software, templates, documentation, brand and know-how;
- the customer receives only the contractual right to use the subscribed service during the applicable term;
- third-party and open-source components remain subject to their applicable licences.

No provision should represent unqualified ownership by the supplier until the creator-to-seller chain of title is legally sufficient and evidenced.

## 10. Acceptable use

The customer must not use the service to compromise security, access another tenant, violate law, infringe third-party rights, introduce malicious content or use unsupported workflows in a manner that creates material security or legal risk.

Any AI Act prohibited-practice wording must remain aligned with the product's actual role and qualified legal interpretation.

## 11. Warranties

The final contract may include a limited commitment that the service will be provided materially in accordance with the applicable documentation, subject to stated exclusions.

This review draft intentionally does not create:
- a blanket compliance warranty;
- uninterrupted/error-free service warranty;
- default uptime/service-credit promise;
- legal-advice warranty.

Final disclaimer mechanics require qualified review.

## 12. Liability

Owner-selected baseline for review: aggregate liability cap equal to fees paid or payable for the affected service in the preceding 12 months, with indirect/consequential loss exclusions to the lawful extent and appropriate carve-outs for non-limitable liability.

This position is not legally effective until counsel confirms mandatory-law treatment, data/confidentiality/IP handling, insurance alignment and negotiated Enterprise exceptions.

## 13. Indemnities

No broad open-ended self-service indemnity is created by this draft. Enterprise provider-IP or other negotiated indemnities, defence-control procedures, exclusions and caps must be expressly stated in the signed Order Form or schedule.

## 14. Termination

The final agreement may permit termination for uncured material breach following an agreed cure period, with immediate rights for non-curable unlawful/security abuse or other defined severe events.

Post-termination export, deletion and retention must remain consistent with verified product/provider capability, the DPA, lawful retention and the Order Form.

## 15. Support and incidents

Applicable support, service and incident commitments are defined by the Order Form and the Service/Support/Incident Schedule.

No stricter response time, breach-notification deadline, 24/7 staffing level, uptime, RTO or RPO is incorporated unless expressly contracted and evidence-backed.

## 16. Subprocessors and transfers

Subprocessor authorisation, notice/objection and international transfer controls are governed by the DPA and current provider/transfer evidence.

Provider framework participation or SCC availability does not by itself establish that every customer flow satisfies GDPR Chapter V.

## 17. Changes

Material contractual changes must follow the final agreed amendment/change mechanism. Product documentation or website copy must not silently expand signed contractual commitments.

## 18. Governing law and disputes

Owner-selected baseline for legal review: Portuguese law and courts in Lisbon, subject to mandatory law and negotiated Enterprise exceptions. This is not final until qualified legal confirmation.

## 19. Notices

The current corporate email intake is `comercial@risckcomply.com`. Formal notice mechanics and postal address remain subject to authoritative registry facts and final legal drafting.

## 20. Entire agreement and execution

The signed Order Form, MSA, DPA and expressly incorporated schedules form the intended agreement for the subscribed services.

Electronic-signature/acceptance mechanics, authority, counterparts and effective date must be confirmed before execution.

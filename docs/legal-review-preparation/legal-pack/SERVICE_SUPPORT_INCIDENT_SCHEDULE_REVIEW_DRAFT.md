# Service, Support and Incident Schedule — Review Draft

**Status:** `REVIEW_DRAFT` · `FOUNDER_FACT_REQUIRED` · `COUNSEL_DECISION_REQUIRED`

This schedule converts operational capabilities into conservative contract language. No hard commitment applies until supported by production evidence and signed.

## 1. Service availability

Default public position: no guaranteed uptime percentage. Any enterprise uptime commitment, measurement method, exclusions, maintenance treatment, service credit and sole-remedy clause are **[FOUNDER FACT AND COUNSEL DECISION REQUIRED]**.

Provider outages, customer systems, force majeure, beta features, misuse and emergency security actions require defined treatment.

## 2. Support channels and hours

Support email/portal, supported languages, business hours, holidays, plan eligibility and escalation paths are **[FOUNDER FACT REQUIRED]**. Security reports should use the dedicated security contact and must not be routed solely through general support.

## 3. Priority model

| Priority | Draft definition | Target response | Target updates |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | service unavailable, confirmed cross-tenant exposure or severe security incident | Founder fact required | Founder fact required |
| P2 High | major feature unavailable or material customer impact without complete outage | Founder fact required | Founder fact required |
| P3 Normal | degraded/non-critical issue with workaround | Founder fact required | Founder fact required |
| P4 Request | question, configuration or feature request | Founder fact required | As appropriate |

Targets are response/update goals, not resolution guarantees, unless expressly agreed.

## 4. Incident handling

The provider will triage, contain, investigate, remediate and document incidents according to available runbooks. Customer communication should state known facts, impact, mitigation and next update without premature root-cause claims.

Personal-data breach notification terms belong in the DPA and must be consistent with this schedule.

## 5. Maintenance

Planned maintenance notice, emergency maintenance rights, maintenance windows and excluded downtime are **[FOUNDER FACT AND COUNSEL DECISION REQUIRED]**.

## 6. Backups, restore and continuity

No recovery point, recovery time or tested restore commitment applies unless a current production artifact proves it and the commitment is listed in the order form. Provider-managed backups and logs may have separate retention and restoration constraints.

## 7. Service credits

**[COUNSEL DECISION REQUIRED]** Define eligibility, claim window, calculation, cap, exclusions and whether credits are the sole remedy for an availability breach. Do not offer credits without billing operations able to administer them.

## 8. Customer responsibilities

Customer must maintain authorised contacts, secure accounts, supply timely diagnostic information, follow supported configurations and implement customer-controlled continuity measures. Customer remains responsible for downstream use of compliance outputs.

## 9. Evidence and review

Before signature, operations must attach the current deployment smoke, observability, rollback, incident-response, backup/restore and provider-status evidence. Counsel must ensure commitments do not exceed that evidence.

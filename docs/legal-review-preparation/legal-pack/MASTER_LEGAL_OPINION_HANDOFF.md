## 4. Questions for the opinion

Counsel should state, with reasons and conditions:

- whether the preliminary product-role and risk classification is defensible;
- whether legal sources and application dates are current and correctly operationalised;
- which workflows are acceptable as decision support and which require changes;
- whether limitations and escalation points are sufficiently clear;
- whether the contract/privacy pack is suitable for the intended launch model;
- which claims are acceptable, qualified or prohibited;
- which issues require local counsel, customer facts, technical specialists or conformity bodies;
- whether any issue blocks launch, enterprise sale or a specific customer/use case.

## 5. Required opinion structure

1. Executive conclusion.
2. Scope, assumptions and documents reviewed.
3. Reviewer identity, qualification, jurisdiction, conflicts and independence.
4. Product role and intended-purpose analysis.
5. Article/workstream findings.
6. Contract, privacy and claims findings.
7. Required changes and conditions precedent.
8. Exclusions, limitations and non-reliance.
9. Validity, change triggers and re-review requirements.
10. Signed decision artifact and digest.

## 6. Mandatory evidence binding

The opinion must identify the exact product SHA, evidence-package digest and signed artifact. A later material product, legal-source, contract, provider, region or claim change invalidates reliance until re-reviewed.

- customer-specific data and use cases;
- external specialist/conformity evidence where applicable.

## 8. Decision outcomes

Use only:

- `ACCEPTED` — within the stated scope and conditions;
- `ACCEPTED_WITH_CHANGES` — credit only after specified changes are verified on the exact resulting SHA;
- `NOT_ACCEPTED` — blocking issue;
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE` — separate advice/evidence required.

No decision may be inferred from silence, package completeness or CI success.

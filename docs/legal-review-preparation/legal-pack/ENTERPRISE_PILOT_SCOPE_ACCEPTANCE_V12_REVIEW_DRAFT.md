## 3. Proposed bounded scope (all features require exact-release verification)

This is a **negotiable evaluation plan**, not a representation that each feature already passed runtime acceptance.

1. Provision one authorised pilot organisation/workspace and the agreed role matrix (owner/admin/member or other genuinely deployed roles).
2. Record a mutually agreed subset of the customer's AI systems, intended purposes, owners and supporting evidence.
3. Exercise risk-screening and assessment/reassessment workflows; labels are decision support, not regulator determinations.
4. Review governance tasks, documentary outputs, export/reload and audit-event evidence **only where operationally verified**.
5. Review access restrictions, tenant switching, retention settings and privacy-assistance process as agreed.
6. Conduct a documented security/procurement handoff with exact deployed SHA, vendor facts and limitations.
7. Where explicitly contracted and operating: verify plan entitlements, quotas, invoice/customer workspace mapping and cancellation terms. **Stripe TEST simulations are not proof of customer payment.**

Exclude by default: unlawful or prohibited use cases; special-category, criminal-offence or children's personal data; bulk production imports; regulated conformity certification; a statutory legal opinion; model training rights; unrestricted third-party penetration testing; unapproved external integrations. Amendments require factual security/legal review and a mutually agreed scope.

## 5. Evaluation sequence and stop conditions

**Preparation:** supplier facts, lawful contract package, authorised pilot owner, agreed data minimisation, exact-release technical verification.  
**Kickoff:** approve use cases, excluded data, roles, responsibilities, evaluation dates and evidence collection.  
**Execution:** documented weekly reviews or a mutually agreed cadence; log defects and scope deviations; do not silently broaden data access.  
**Decision:** customer confirms actual observed outcomes, outstanding defects and any paid continuation in a separately approved commercial decision. A pilot cannot auto-renew based on an internal green dashboard alone.

Stop or suspend as lawfully required if tenant isolation fails, billing is materially inconsistent, customer data is exposed, a material incident occurs, processor terms are absent, or the agreed scope cannot be honoured. Incident notifications follow applicable law and the effective DPA; this draft promises no invented 24/7 service, uptime percentage, RTO/RPO or security certification.

## 6. Regulatory and data-processing boundary

RISCK COMPLY organises AI governance information; it does not substitute for customer legal classification, provider/deployer duties, conformity assessment, DPIA, FRIA or regulator action. Determine applicable EU AI Act obligations by actual system, role and date, including the **27 July 2026 amended framework**, not the original 2024 transition calendar alone. GDPR rights, subprocessors, international transfers and retention depend on verified data flows and a lawful effective contract.

# High-Risk Provider Controls — Counsel Review Package

**Status:** `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` · **Weight:** 9

Review the provider-side methodology for Articles 9–15: risk management, data governance, provenance, representativeness, bias, logging, instructions, human oversight, accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity.

## Critical boundaries

- Workspace completion cannot establish legal high-risk classification.
- Checklist completion cannot prove dataset adequacy, performance or conformity.
- Actual intended purpose, sector and deployment facts remain external.
- Technical or domain-specialist review may be required in addition to legal review.

## Decisions required

Confirm control coverage, minimum evidence, specialist-escalation triggers, amended application timelines and all limitation language. The accepted result must be bound to the exact SHA and stored through the registered decision path.

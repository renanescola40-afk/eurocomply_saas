## 9. ROI / TCO calculator

All variables must remain editable. Never hard-code invented customer economics.

### Customer inputs

- annual governance/compliance staff cost affected: `C_staff`
- external advisory/audit spend potentially avoided or reduced: `C_external`
- evidence/document preparation hours per year: `H_evidence`
- fully loaded hourly cost: `R_hour`
- expected reduction in evidence/document hours: `P_time_reduction`
- duplicate-tool cost potentially retired: `C_tools`
- annual RISCK COMPLY subscription: `C_risck`
- implementation/pilot cost: `C_impl`

### Annual gross benefit

`TIME_SAVING = H_evidence * R_hour * P_time_reduction`

`GROSS_BENEFIT = TIME_SAVING + C_external_reduction + C_tools_reduction + other_attributable_savings`

### First-year net benefit

`NET_BENEFIT_Y1 = GROSS_BENEFIT - C_risck - C_impl`

### Steady-state annual net benefit

`NET_BENEFIT_STEADY = GROSS_BENEFIT - C_risck`

### ROI

`ROI_Y1 = NET_BENEFIT_Y1 / (C_risck + C_impl)`

### Payback period

`PAYBACK_MONTHS = ((C_risck + C_impl) / GROSS_BENEFIT) * 12`

Do not monetize regulatory penalties as guaranteed avoided cost. If risk reduction is discussed, label it as scenario analysis, not certain savings.

# RISCK COMPLY — Enterprise Commercial Sequence V1

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: prepared / no outreach sent by this artifact

## Objective

Convert target hypotheses into attributable economic evidence without changing core product code:

`TARGET -> DISCOVERY -> QUALIFIED OPPORTUNITY -> LOI/PILOT INTENT -> PAID PILOT -> ANNUAL CONTRACT`

## 1. Buyer personas

Prioritize roles according to the real organization/use case rather than sending the same message to everyone.

Primary personas:

- Chief Risk Officer / Enterprise Risk;
- Compliance / Regulatory Affairs;
- General Counsel / Technology Legal;
- DPO / Privacy;
- CISO / Security Governance;
- CIO / CTO for governance/platform ownership;
- Head of Data / AI / Responsible AI;
- Internal Audit;
- Procurement / Third-Party Risk after sponsor qualification.

## 2. Discovery qualification

A discovery is qualified only when the following can be recorded truthfully:

- organization;
- named/attributable contact;
- role;
- use case;
- governance/compliance trigger;
- approximate number/range of AI systems;
- approximate legal entities/business units;
- existing process/tooling;
- pain or risk of current process;
- timing;
- sponsor/decision path;
- security/legal/procurement route;
- pilot fit;
- indicative ACV or budget conversation status;
- explicit next step and date.

## 3. First-touch positioning

Core positioning:

**RISCK COMPLY is an enterprise AI governance and compliance evidence platform for organizations that need a defensible inventory, risk/classification process, governance workflow, evidence and audit-ready documentation across AI use cases.**

Truth boundaries:

- do not guarantee legal compliance;
- do not claim regulator approval;
- do not claim customers/ARR not evidenced;
- do not claim certifications not held;
- do not claim a clean independent pentest/retest before terminal evidence;
- do not imply the recipient has a compliance failure without evidence.

## 4. Outreach sequence — commercial template

### Touch 1 — problem / relevance

Purpose: test whether AI inventory/governance/evidence is a live issue.

Message structure:

1. one line identifying RISCK COMPLY;
2. one line on the target's sector/regulatory context without pretending inside knowledge;
3. one concise problem statement: fragmented AI inventory/governance/evidence;
4. one product outcome statement;
5. one low-friction ask: whether the organization is evaluating AI-governance tooling/processes and who owns that area.

### Touch 2 — enterprise evidence angle

Purpose: distinguish the platform from a checklist/tool-only pitch.

Focus:

- AI system inventory;
- risk/classification workflow;
- evidence/documentation;
- role-based governance;
- audit/procurement readiness;
- enterprise security architecture.

### Touch 3 — bounded paid pilot

Purpose: give a concrete commercial path.

Default offer:

`6-week AI Governance Readiness Pilot — indicative EUR 10k, bounded scope`

Do not offer a free bespoke implementation as the default.

### Touch 4 — procurement path

For qualified prospects only:

- provide buyer-safe security/privacy/procurement packet;
- identify questionnaire/DPA/security review process;
- record blockers and owner;
- do not fabricate certification to accelerate procurement.

## 5. Qualification gates

### Gate A — TARGET -> CONTACT_IDENTIFIED

Evidence:

- legitimate public/corporate contact source;
- role is relevant.

### Gate B — CONTACTED -> DISCOVERY

Evidence:

- response or scheduled/accepted discovery exchange;
- no credit for messages merely sent.

### Gate C — DISCOVERY -> QUALIFIED

Evidence:

- real use case;
- timing/problem;
- decision path;
- scope estimate;
- next step.

### Gate D — QUALIFIED -> LOI_OR_PILOT_INTENT

Evidence:

- attributable written statement;
- intended scope;
- indicative commercial path;
- timing/conditions.

### Gate E — PILOT -> PAID_PILOT

Evidence:

- signed order/contract or legitimate payment commitment;
- no synthetic Stripe/customer objects.

### Gate F — PAID_PILOT -> ANNUAL

Evidence:

- executive outcome/readout;
- agreed annual scope;
- procurement path;
- annual contract/payment authority.

## 6. LOI acceptance standard

Count toward valuation only when the written evidence contains:

- organization identity;
- sponsor/decision owner or attributable authorized contact;
- defined use case;
- intended scope;
- pilot and/or annual subscription intent;
- indicative commercial range or explicit procurement/budget path;
- target timing;
- conditions;
- signatory/attribution.

A generic 'interested in learning more' email is not a valuation-grade LOI.

## 7. Pipeline economics

Use scenario math, not claimed forecast.

Example planning model only:

- 40 target accounts;
- 20% reach a substantive discovery = 8;
- 50% of discoveries qualify = 4;
- 50% of qualified opportunities enter paid pilot/LOI = 2;
- 50% of pilots convert annual = 1 annual customer.

These percentages are hypotheses until RISCK COMPLY has its own observed funnel data.

## 9. Partner sequence

For law/GRC/cyber/AI consulting partners:

1. validate client overlap;
2. determine referral vs reseller vs implementation role;
3. preserve separation between software and legal advice;
4. pilot with one joint client when legitimate;
5. measure sourced pipeline and conversion;
6. only then claim partner-channel traction.

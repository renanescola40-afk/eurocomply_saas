Public deal announcements describe FairNow capabilities including an AI registry, dynamic risk assessments and automated compliance guidance. Transaction value was not publicly disclosed in the sources reviewed.

Public source examples:

- https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/auditboard-announces-acquisition-of-fairnow-to-expand-ai-governance-capabilities-882469365.html
- https://www.themiddlemarket.com/latest-news/auditboard-purchases-ai-governance-platform-fairnow

### RISCK COMPLY fit hypothesis

Potential adjacency:

- AI inventory;
- AI risk/classification;
- compliance/evidence workflows;
- enterprise governance;
- auditability;
- EU AI Act-first positioning.

### Why buy rather than build — hypothesis

A specialized acquisition can accelerate regional regulatory depth, evidence workflows and engineering already built around EU AI Act/GDPR/enterprise assurance rather than starting a separate European product line from zero.

### Likely objections / diligence

- pre-revenue status;
- no proven customer base;
- overlap with FairNow or internal roadmap;
- exact-SHA Production not yet terminally closed;
- IP chain-of-title/data-room completion;
- seller/entity/tax facts;
- independent assurance depth.

### What would materially improve fit

- 3–5 qualified European LOIs;
- regulated-industry paid pilots;
- repeatable evidence of EU-specific governance demand;
- proprietary regulatory/evidence mapping depth;
- clean acquisition IP pack;
- exact-SHA Enterprise closure.

## 6. Cross-acquirer value narrative

The acquisition case must never be 'we wrote a lot of code.'

Use four evidence-backed questions:

1. How many months of product/regulatory engineering does RISCK COMPLY save?
2. What EU customer segment or channel does it accelerate entry into?
3. Which governance/evidence workflows are genuinely differentiated from the acquirer's existing stack?
4. What attributable commercial proof shows the market values those differences?

## 7. Evidence required before acquisition outreach carries serious weight

Priority evidence stack:

1. accepted exact-SHA Production release;
2. authoritative seller/entity/tax facts;
3. acquisition-safe IP/data-room package;
4. 3–5 qualified LOIs, ideally regulated enterprises;
5. 1–3 paid pilots or annual customers;
6. repeatable pilot-to-annual conversion path;
7. attributable independent security assurance where relevant;
8. documented partner/channel leverage;
9. measurable customer ROI/time-to-evidence benefit;
10. defensible regulatory/evidence mapping IP.

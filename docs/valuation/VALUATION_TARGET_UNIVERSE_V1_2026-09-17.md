## D. Qualification sequence

No target moves into qualified pipeline until all material steps below are attributable.

1. Verify organization still matches target profile.
2. Identify a legitimate business contact source.
3. Confirm role/persona without guessing authority.
4. Establish actual AI-governance/compliance use case.
5. Confirm problem urgency or timing trigger.
6. Estimate systems/entities/users in scope.
7. Establish pilot fit and indicative commercial range.
8. Record security/legal/procurement requirements.
9. Record explicit next step and date.
10. Only then assign `QUALIFIED` and an internal indicative ACV.

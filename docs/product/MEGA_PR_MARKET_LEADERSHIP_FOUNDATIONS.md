# Mega PR — Market Leadership Foundations

## Objective

Consolidate the first defensible product moat for RISCK COMPLY around five reusable foundations:

1. AI governance digital twin;
2. normalized controls and cross-framework mappings;
3. evidence-by-design contracts;
4. regulatory change impact analysis;
5. guided onboarding and measurable time-to-value.

## Truth boundary

This work creates product foundations and verifiable contracts. It does not by itself prove market leadership, legal compliance, production scale, customer adoption, retention, or external assurance.

## Delivery gates

- tenant-scoped data model and RLS;
- central domain contracts;
- API authorization and validation;
- deterministic impact and evidence services;
- tests for isolation, integrity and expiry;
- documented analytics events;
- no unsupported commercial claims.

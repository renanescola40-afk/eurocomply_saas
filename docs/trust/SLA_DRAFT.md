# Service Level Agreement (SLA) draft

Status: draft for commercial and legal review. Do not offer as a contractual SLA until monitoring, incident response, and support coverage are operationally ready.

## Purpose

Define a future enterprise SLA framework for EuroComply customers.

## Draft service availability target

| Plan / tier | Draft target | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-service | Best effort | No contractual SLA |
| Enterprise | 99.5% - 99.9% draft range | Not yet contractually offered |

## Exclusions draft

Potential exclusions include:

- Scheduled maintenance with advance notice.
- Customer-caused downtime or misconfiguration.
- Third-party provider outages outside EuroComply control.
- Force majeure events.
- Beta features and non-production environments.
- Security suspensions or abuse prevention actions.

## Support response targets draft

| Severity | Example | Draft initial response target | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Production unavailable for all users | 4 business hours / future 24x7 target | Not contractually offered |
| P2 | Major feature unavailable | 1 business day | Not contractually offered |
| P3 | Non-critical defect | 2 business days | Not contractually offered |
| P4 | General question | 3 business days | Not contractually offered |

## Required before customer commitment

1. Production uptime monitor.
2. Alert routing and escalation policy.
3. Incident response owner rotation.
4. Status page or incident communications process.
5. Monthly uptime reporting.
6. Legal-approved credit/remedy terms.
7. Provider dependency review.

## Customer-safe answer while draft

EuroComply does not currently offer a contractual SLA. A draft enterprise SLA framework exists and will be offered only after monitoring, incident response, and legal terms are finalized.

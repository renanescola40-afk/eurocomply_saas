# Legal Safe Messaging

Date: 2026-07-06
Brand: RISCK COMPLY

## Principle

Sell confidence, structure and operational readiness. Do not sell legal certainty.

RISCK COMPLY may say it helps teams create an AI system inventory, map owners and workflows, improve risk visibility, prepare governance evidence, organize documents and support customer, security and procurement conversations.

## Primary tagline

AI governance readiness for European B2B teams.

## Primary value proposition

RISCK COMPLY helps European companies organize AI inventory, risk visibility, governance workflows and evidence preparation for AI Act readiness.

## Standard disclaimer

RISCK COMPLY supports organization, workflows and evidence preparation. It does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee compliance outcomes.

## Approved CTA language

- Join waitlist
- Request access
- Request demo
- Compare plans
- Start readiness review
- Open AI inventory
- Prepare evidence
- Review risk signals
- Invite your team

## Plan names

Customer-facing plan names should be Starter, Growth and Enterprise. Legacy aliases can remain in code for billing compatibility, but should not be visible to customers.

## Trust language

Approved trust signals include authenticated workspaces, organization-scoped access, role-based access patterns, audit activity for relevant events, responsible disclosure, subprocessor register, security review support and formal external assurance pending.

## Enterprise language

Enterprise pages can describe plan scope, collaboration, support and review support. They should not imply universal readiness for every procurement or regulated environment.

Recommended wording:

> Advanced governance support for teams that need more users, longer audit history, higher limits and priority support.

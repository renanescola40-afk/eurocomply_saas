# Data protection

Status: enterprise data-protection overview. This document is not a legal privacy policy, DPA or certification statement.

EuroComply may process account data, organization data, compliance records, controlled documents, audit metadata, billing metadata and operational logs depending on customer configuration.

The product is designed around organization-scoped access, RBAC, tenant-boundary controls, private document handling and audit logging. Retention, deletion, export and subprocessor commitments must be finalized in the applicable customer agreement.

# Data Processing Addendum (DPA) draft

Status: draft for legal review. Do not send or sign this document without qualified legal review.

## Purpose

This DPA draft defines the minimum data protection terms RISCK COMPLY must be ready to offer when processing personal data on behalf of business customers.

## Parties

- Customer: the data controller or business customer using RISCK COMPLY.
- RISCK COMPLY: the processor/service provider operating the SaaS platform.

## Scope

RISCK COMPLY processes customer data only to provide, secure, support, and improve the contracted SaaS service, subject to the customer agreement and documented instructions.

## Categories of data

Expected categories include:

- Account and user profile data.
- Organization membership and role data.
- Compliance documents uploaded by customers.
- Audit events and operational metadata.
- Billing and subscription metadata.
- Support communications, if provided by the customer.

## Security measures

Current technical measures include organization-scoped access controls, server-side permission checks, secure headers, trusted-origin checks, document upload validation, audit events, CI security checks, and internal cron authentication. Formal evidence must be collected for production tenant isolation, backup restore, disaster recovery, and audit retention.

## Subprocessors

RISCK COMPLY must maintain a subprocessors register before signing this DPA. Customers should be notified of material subprocessors changes according to the final contract.

## Data subject requests

RISCK COMPLY should assist customers with access, deletion, export, and correction requests where technically feasible. The support workflow and response SLA must be finalized before offering contractual commitments.

## Breach notification

A final DPA must define breach notification timing, required content, contact channel, and escalation responsibilities. Do not commit to a notification deadline until legal and operational readiness are confirmed.

## Deletion and return

Customer data should be deleted or returned at the end of service according to the retention policy and legal requirements. Implementation evidence must be maintained.

## Open legal items

- SCCs or other transfer mechanism, if needed.
- Controller/processor terminology by region.
- Liability limits and indemnity alignment.
- Subprocessor notice period.
- Breach notification timeline.
- Customer audit rights.

Provide one truthful, reusable response when a buyer asks for CSA CAIQ / STAR cloud-security evidence without confusing the existing CSA STAR for AI Level 1 AI-CAIQ listing with the separate standard cloud-security CAIQ v4.1 questionnaire.

RISCK COMPLY has a publicly verified CSA STAR for AI Level 1 entry based on the AI-CAIQ v1.1.0 self-assessment. This is a Level 1 self-assessment/transparency designation and is not independent certification.

The separate standard CSA STAR Level 1 cloud-security CAIQ v4.1 questionnaire is not currently represented as completed, submitted, accepted or listed. No buyer answer may imply otherwise.

## Safe buyer wording

> RISCK COMPLY has published a CSA STAR for AI Level 1 AI-CAIQ v1.1.0 self-assessment. Our separate standard cloud-security CAIQ v4.1 submission is not currently represented as completed or listed. For security diligence we can provide our enterprise security questionnaire, architecture/security documentation and evidence-mapped control materials, with detailed evidence shared according to confidentiality and need-to-know.

## Restricted wording

Do not use:

- `CSA certified`;
- `CSA approved`;
- `independently audited by CSA`;
- `standard CSA STAR Level 1 listed` unless a live standard STAR entry is later verified;
- `CAIQ v4.1 completed` until the actual questionnaire is completed and evidence-reconciled;
- `ISO 27001 certified`;
- `SOC 2 certified`;
- `EU AI Act certified`.

## Questionnaire handling rule

If a buyer supplies its own CAIQ v4.1 workbook or exact questionnaire version:

1. answer only fields supported by current canonical evidence;
2. use `UNKNOWN / FACT_REQUIRED` rather than guessing;
3. identify the source document/evidence for each material answer;
4. distinguish design/source controls from current production evidence;
5. distinguish provider control from RISCK COMPLY control;
6. do not convert an inherited provider certification into a RISCK COMPLY certification;
7. retain the completed buyer-specific workbook as controlled diligence evidence rather than changing canonical policy merely to satisfy the questionnaire.

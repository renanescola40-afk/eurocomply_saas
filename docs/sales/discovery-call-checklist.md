# RISCK COMPLY — Discovery Call Checklist

Use this checklist before and during a discovery call. The goal is to identify the first high-value workflow and qualify enterprise fit.

## 1. Company context

- Company name:
- Region/countries:
- Industry:
- Employee count:
- Number of entities:
- Main buyer/persona:
- Decision timeline:

## 2. Current process

Ask:

- Where are risks tracked today?
- Where are compliance documents stored?
- Where is vendor evidence stored?
- How are review dates managed?
- Who owns audit or customer evidence requests?
- What breaks most often?

Score current process:

- 1 = mostly ad hoc.
- 2 = spreadsheets and folders.
- 3 = some workflow, still fragmented.
- 4 = structured but not centralized.
- 5 = mature system, clear owners and evidence.

## 3. Pain priority

Rank from 1 to 5:

- Risk register.
- Document control.
- Vendor review.
- Audit readiness.
- Executive reporting.
- GDPR-oriented evidence.
- Customer procurement reviews.

## 4. Business trigger

Identify the trigger:

- Upcoming audit.
- Enterprise customer procurement.
- GDPR/vendor review pressure.
- New compliance owner.
- Fundraising or board reporting.
- Expansion to new country.
- Too many manual spreadsheets.

## 5. Data readiness

Can they provide:

- Existing risk spreadsheet.
- Vendor list.
- Document inventory.
- Compliance owner list.
- Recent audit/customer questions.

## 6. Decision process

Ask:

- Who signs the budget?
- Who owns implementation?
- Who reviews privacy/security?
- Is procurement required?
- Is a DPA required?
- Do they need a security questionnaire?

## 7. Success criteria

Define the first success milestone:

- Replace one spreadsheet.
- Build vendor register.
- Add top 10 documents.
- Assign owners and deadlines.
- Create first evidence pack.
- Prepare executive dashboard.

## 8. Qualification

Good fit when:

- More than one person owns compliance work.
- They use spreadsheets today.
- Vendors need recurring review.
- Customers ask for evidence.
- Leadership wants visibility.
- They have a clear deadline or trigger.

Poor fit when:

- No active compliance process.
- No owner.
- No budget or business trigger.
- They only want legal advice.
- They need a certified GRC platform immediately and RISCK COMPLY evidence does not match the requirement yet.

## 9. Close

End with:

“Based on what you shared, the first workflow I would map is [workflow]. If we can centralize that in RISCK COMPLY, the first measurable outcome would be [outcome]. The next step is a 30-minute demo with [stakeholders].”

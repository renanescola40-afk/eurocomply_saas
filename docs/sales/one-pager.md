# RISCK COMPLY — One-Pager

## The European compliance control room

RISCK COMPLY helps companies manage risks, documents, vendors, audit evidence and compliance ownership in one place.

It is built for teams that have outgrown spreadsheets and need a clearer way to prove who owns what, what is missing and what is ready for review.

## Who it is for

- CFOs who need visibility over compliance work and operational risk.
- Compliance Managers who need ownership, deadlines and evidence.
- DPOs who need clearer vendor and data-processing visibility.
- Founders and COOs who need enterprise readiness before customer reviews.
- B2B vendors that need a stronger procurement answer.

## What RISCK COMPLY centralizes

- Risk register with severity, owner, status and mitigation.
- Controlled documents with ownership, review dates and version context.
- Vendor register with criticality, DPA status and evidence gaps.
- Compliance actions with deadlines and accountable owners.
- Audit trail and evidence packs for internal review and buyer questions.
- Executive view of what is missing, overdue or high risk.

## Why now

European buyers increasingly expect structured answers around privacy, vendors, controls, policies and evidence. Manual processes work at the beginning, but they become fragile when the company grows, sells to larger clients or prepares for review.

## Business value

- Reduce manual spreadsheet updates and owner chasing.
- Improve visibility for leadership.
- Prepare faster for audits, procurement and customer reviews.
- Reduce the risk of missing documents, vendor evidence or review dates.
- Create a repeatable operating model for compliance.

## Positioning

**RISCK COMPLY helps European companies control compliance work across risks, documents, vendors, audit evidence and accountability — without running the business from spreadsheets.**

## Sales rules

- Do not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001 certification, third-party pentest completion or guaranteed legal compliance unless the signed contract and Trust Center evidence explicitly support it.
- Do not present RISCK COMPLY as legal advice. Position it as an operational compliance system for evidence, workflow, visibility and readiness.
- Enterprise commitments must be tied to contract, DPA, subprocessor list, support terms and security evidence.

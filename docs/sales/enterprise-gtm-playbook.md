## 4. Main pain

AI usage is spreading faster than governance.

Teams are using ChatGPT, copilots, automations, AI features, third-party AI vendors and internal workflows, but evidence is scattered across Slack, spreadsheets, Notion, Google Drive, emails and deck screenshots.

The result:

- unclear owners;

- policies disconnected from real tools;

## 11. Objections and responses

### “Can this make us compliant with the EU AI Act?”

No software should promise that alone. RISCK COMPLY helps you organize the operational layer: AI inventory, owners, risk, policies, evidence and review preparation. Legal interpretation remains with your legal counsel and advisors.

### “We already have spreadsheets.”

That is exactly the starting point. Spreadsheets work until ownership, evidence, risk status, review history and procurement answers need to stay consistent across teams. RISCK COMPLY gives that process a controlled system of record.

### “We are too early for this.”

If AI is already used in your company, you are not too early. Start with inventory and ownership now, then mature policy, risk and evidence over time.

### “Our lawyers handle compliance.”

Legal counsel is important. RISCK COMPLY does not replace them. It gives the business a working evidence system so legal, compliance, security and product teams are not operating from disconnected documents.

### “We need SOC 2 or ISO 27001 from vendors.”

Current certifications must be represented honestly in the Trust Center and procurement materials. RISCK COMPLY can share available security documentation and roadmap items, but unsupported certifications are not claimed.

### “Why not use a large GRC platform?”

Large GRC platforms are broad and heavy. RISCK COMPLY is focused on AI governance readiness for European B2B teams that need fast inventory, risk, policy and evidence workflows.

### “Can we export our data?”

Position exports as plan-dependent. Public copy should say: “Export capabilities are available according to plan and procurement agreement.”

### “What if AI Act timelines change?”

The value remains: companies still need inventory, ownership, risk visibility, policy discipline and evidence. RISCK COMPLY is built around governance readiness, not a single deadline slogan.

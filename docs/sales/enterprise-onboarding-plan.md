# RISCK COMPLY — Enterprise Onboarding Plan

Status: `BUYER_READY_INTERNAL / CONTRACT_TIMELINE_BY_SCOPE`

Use this plan after commercial qualification and before production rollout. It is a buyer-facing implementation framework, not a fixed SLA.

## Phase 0 — Commercial and trust handoff

Inputs:

- buyer sponsor and operational owner;
- intended teams/entities/countries;
- selected commercial plan or enterprise scope;
- procurement/security requirements;
- applicable DPA/order/support requirements.

Exit:

- named buyer sponsor;
- named implementation owner;
- agreed initial scope;
- open procurement items listed.

## Phase 1 — Kickoff and success criteria

Agree the first measurable outcome. Examples:

- establish the initial AI-system inventory;
- replace one governance spreadsheet;
- complete a baseline risk/classification review;
- prepare one review-ready evidence pack;
- establish owners and review cadence.

Do not promise regulatory approval or automatic compliance as a success criterion.

## Phase 2 — Workspace and access model

- create/confirm organization workspace;
- identify owner/admin/member responsibilities;
- invite initial users;
- validate access and tenant boundaries for the rollout;
- agree who may manage billing, members, evidence and settings.

## Phase 3 — Initial AI inventory

Customer provides a starting inventory or enters systems manually.

Recommended minimum fields:

- system/use-case name;
- business purpose;
- owner/team;
- provider/vendor;
- deployment/use geography where relevant;
- data/context notes;
- lifecycle/review status.

Start with a bounded set rather than importing unreviewed bulk data.

## Phase 4 — Assessment and governance baseline

For the agreed initial systems:

- run the applicable assessment/classification workflow;
- review owners and lifecycle state;
- record risks/actions;
- attach or generate relevant governance documentation;
- record evidence gaps without converting unknowns into PASS.

## Phase 5 — Documents, evidence and monitoring

- establish the initial document/evidence set;
- assign review ownership;
- configure monitoring/review preferences where applicable;
- confirm which outputs are operational records versus legal/advisory inputs;
- prepare the first internal/buyer review pack.

## Phase 6 — Procurement and security handoff

Provide the evidence-bound buyer materials as required:

- Trust Center;
- Security Questionnaire;
- Architecture Overview;
- DPA/subprocessor/transfer review surfaces;
- Procurement Pack;
- applicable controlled data-room references.

Confidential reports or provider-account evidence are shared only through an approved controlled channel when genuinely required.

## Phase 7 — Training and operational handover

Cover:

- adding/updating AI systems;
- reassessment and lifecycle changes;
- document/evidence workflows;
- monitoring and review ownership;
- member/role administration;
- support/escalation route;
- buyer/customer review workflow.

## Phase 8 — Expansion review

After the initial use case is stable, decide whether to expand by:

- additional teams;
- additional entities/countries;
- additional AI systems;
- more evidence/document workflows;
- additional governance owners;
- contract/support scope.

## Customer input checklist

Before kickoff, ask the customer to bring:

- current AI tools/system list;
- current AI policy/governance documents;
- known owners/teams;
- priority use cases;
- current risk/compliance spreadsheet if one exists;
- relevant buyer/board/audit questions;
- procurement/security requirements;
- named implementation sponsor.

## Timeline language

Buyer-safe wording:

> A focused first workspace can be established quickly when the customer has a usable AI inventory and named owners. Enterprise rollout timing depends on the number of teams, systems, data quality, migration work, procurement requirements and agreed onboarding scope. Any fixed implementation timeline or service commitment belongs in the signed agreement.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] sponsor and implementation owner named
- [ ] initial scope agreed
- [ ] workspace/access model established
- [ ] initial inventory loaded
- [ ] baseline assessment completed
- [ ] first evidence/document set prepared
- [ ] monitoring/review ownership established
- [ ] security/procurement handoff completed where required
- [ ] team trained
- [ ] expansion decision recorded

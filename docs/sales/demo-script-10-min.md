# RISCK COMPLY — 10-Minute Demo Script

Audience: CFO, Compliance Manager, DPO, COO, Head of Risk, founder or procurement reviewer.

Goal: show that RISCK COMPLY replaces scattered spreadsheets, folders and manual reminders with one operating system for risks, documents, vendors, audit evidence and compliance ownership.

## Demo promise

“In 10 minutes, I will show how RISCK COMPLY helps your team know what is owned, what is missing, what is risky and what is ready for review.”

## Demo setup

Prepare a demo workspace with:

- Company: Acme Europe Ltd.
- Risks: missing DPA, overdue policy review, vendor evidence gap, audit action overdue, critical provider review.
- Documents: Security Policy, Data Processing Addendum, Vendor Register, Internal Compliance Checklist.
- Vendors: payroll provider, analytics tool, cloud hosting provider.
- Dashboard: open actions, high-priority risks and upcoming reviews.

## Flow

### 0:00–1:00 — Pain

Open with:

“Most teams do not fail compliance because nobody cares. They fail because evidence is split between spreadsheets, email, folders and people’s memory.”

Ask:

- Where do you currently track risks, vendors and documents?
- Who owns the final answer when a customer or auditor asks for proof?

### 1:00–2:00 — Executive dashboard

Show overall posture, open actions, high-risk items, upcoming deadlines and recent activity.

Talk track:

“This is the leadership view. It is not only storage. It shows what needs attention, who owns it and whether the company can prove the control exists.”

### 2:00–3:20 — Risk register

Show risk list, severity, owner, status and mitigation notes.

Talk track:

“Every risk becomes an accountable work item with owner, status and evidence.”

### 3:20–4:40 — Document control

Show controlled documents, current version, owner, status and review date.

Talk track:

“The enterprise question is not only ‘do you have a policy?’ It is ‘which version is current, who owns it and when was it reviewed?’”

### 4:40–6:00 — Vendor control

Show vendors, criticality, DPA status, review status and missing evidence.

Talk track:

“Vendor risk is where spreadsheets break fast. RISCK COMPLY helps teams see what must be requested before approval, renewal or customer review.”

### 6:00–7:20 — Audit evidence

Show activity, evidence pack and owner mapping.

Talk track:

“The goal is to answer enterprise questions with evidence, not memory.”

### 7:20–8:30 — Enterprise FAQ moment

Say:

“RISCK COMPLY supports GDPR-oriented workflows, vendor evidence, audit trails, role-based access and managed cloud deployment. Contract terms such as DPA, subprocessors, support and security evidence are handled during enterprise review.”

Do not present RISCK COMPLY as legal advice or claim certifications that are not listed in the Trust Center.

### 8:30–9:20 — ROI

Use:

`Monthly hours saved × blended hourly cost + avoided admin cost + faster review value - subscription cost`

Example:

`30 hours × €60 + €500 avoided admin cost - €399 plan = €1,901 estimated monthly value`

### 9:20–10:00 — Close

Ask:

- Should we map your current process in a 30-minute discovery call?
- Who should join next: finance, DPO, IT/security, procurement or legal?

Close:

“The next step is to map your current workflow, choose the first 3 use cases and decide whether to start with risks, documents, vendors or audit readiness.”

# RISCK COMPLY — Commercial FAQ

Use this FAQ in sales calls, procurement conversations and follow-up emails.

## 1. What does RISCK COMPLY do?

RISCK COMPLY helps European companies centralize compliance operations across risks, controlled documents, vendors, evidence, owners, deadlines and audit preparation.

It is designed to replace fragile spreadsheet-based workflows with a clearer operating system for compliance accountability.

## 2. Is RISCK COMPLY legal advice?

No. RISCK COMPLY is an operational compliance management platform. It helps teams organize evidence, workflows, documents and ownership. It does not replace legal counsel or guarantee legal compliance.

## 3. How does RISCK COMPLY support GDPR-related work?

RISCK COMPLY can support GDPR-oriented operational workflows such as vendor tracking, DPA status, evidence collection, document review cycles, ownership and audit-ready records.

Final legal interpretation and regulatory decisions remain the responsibility of the customer and their advisors.

## 4. What data does RISCK COMPLY process?

Typical data may include company profile information, user account details, compliance records, risk notes, document metadata, vendor records, owner assignments and support/demo contact information.

Customers should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive data unless the agreed plan and controls support that use case.

## 5. Where is data stored?

RISCK COMPLY is designed around managed cloud infrastructure. The exact production region, subprocessors and contractual commitments should be confirmed in the Trust Center, DPA and signed agreement.

## 6. Is data encrypted?

RISCK COMPLY is designed to rely on managed-provider encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the underlying infrastructure. Specific commitments should be verified against the Trust Center and customer contract.

## 7. Does RISCK COMPLY support role-based access?

Yes. RISCK COMPLY is designed around organization workspaces and role-based access patterns so teams can separate owners, admins and members according to their responsibilities.

## 8. Does RISCK COMPLY have audit trails?

RISCK COMPLY is designed to record important workflow activity so teams can review changes, ownership and evidence history.

## 9. Do you have SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

Do not claim completed certifications unless they are present in the live Trust Center and contract pack. If a buyer asks, answer with the current evidence available and the roadmap.

Suggested answer:

“RISCK COMPLY keeps security claims tied to documented evidence. We can share the current Trust Center materials during enterprise review.”

## 10. Do you sign a DPA?

For business and enterprise customers, DPA review can be part of the procurement process. Final terms depend on the signed agreement.

## 11. Do you list subprocessors?

Subprocessor information should be handled through the Trust Center or enterprise procurement pack. Keep this list updated before enterprise sales conversations.

## 12. How does onboarding work?

Typical onboarding:

1. Discovery and current workflow mapping.
2. Workspace setup.
3. Import or manual entry of first risks, vendors and documents.
4. Owner mapping.
5. First dashboard and review cadence.
6. Team training.

For smaller teams, onboarding can be self-serve. For enterprise customers, assisted onboarding can be scoped commercially.

## 13. How long does onboarding take?

A focused first workspace can be ready quickly when the customer has a basic risk list, vendor list and document inventory. Enterprise timelines depend on data quality, number of teams, migration volume and procurement requirements.

## 14. How is pricing structured?

Pricing can be based on company size, number of users, number of countries/entities, document volume, vendor volume, onboarding level and support requirements.

Use public plans for simple buyers and enterprise pricing for larger or regulated teams.

## 15. What support is included?

Support depends on the plan. Enterprise support terms should be defined in the signed agreement, including channels, response targets, onboarding help and review cadence.

## 16. Can RISCK COMPLY replace our spreadsheet immediately?

Yes for a first workflow, if the customer can provide a clear starting point: risk list, vendor list, documents and owners. Start with one use case, prove value, then expand.

## 17. What should we bring to a demo?

Bring:

- Current risk spreadsheet.
- Vendor list.
- Top compliance documents.
- Current audit or customer review questions.
- Main owners: finance, compliance, DPO, IT/security and procurement.

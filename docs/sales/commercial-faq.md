# RISCK COMPLY — Commercial FAQ

Use this FAQ in sales calls, procurement conversations and follow-up emails.

## 1. What does RISCK COMPLY do?

RISCK COMPLY helps European companies organize AI governance work across AI system inventory, risk classification, owners, policies, evidence, review preparation and procurement review.

It is designed to replace fragile spreadsheet-based workflows with a clearer operating system for AI governance accountability.

## 2. Is RISCK COMPLY legal advice?

No. RISCK COMPLY is an operational AI governance readiness platform. It helps teams organize evidence, workflows, documents and ownership. It does not replace legal counsel or guarantee legal compliance.

## 3. Does RISCK COMPLY guarantee EU AI Act compliance?

No. No software should claim automatic legal compliance. RISCK COMPLY helps teams prepare and maintain the operational evidence layer: AI inventory, owners, risk, policies, evidence packs and review preparation.

Final legal interpretation and regulatory decisions remain the responsibility of the customer and their advisors.

## 4. Who is RISCK COMPLY for?

RISCK COMPLY is for European B2B companies using AI in product, operations, HR, support, sales, finance or customer-facing workflows.

Best-fit buyers include founders, CTOs, compliance managers, DPOs, legal operations, security leads and COOs.

## 5. What is the main business problem?

AI usage is spreading faster than governance. Teams often use AI tools, copilots, automations and vendors before the company has a reliable system of record for what is used, who owns it, what risk it carries and what evidence exists.

## 6. How does RISCK COMPLY support EU AI Act readiness?

RISCK COMPLY can support EU AI Act readiness workflows by helping teams:

- register AI systems and use cases;
- assign owners;
- structure risk signals;
- connect policies and documents to real AI systems;
- prepare evidence packs;
- maintain activity history;
- prepare buyer, board, review and procurement answers.

This is operational readiness support, not legal advice.

## 7. How does RISCK COMPLY support GDPR-related work?

RISCK COMPLY can support GDPR-oriented operational workflows such as vendor tracking, DPA status, evidence collection, document review cycles, ownership and review-ready records.

Final legal interpretation and regulatory decisions remain the responsibility of the customer and their advisors.

## 8. What data does RISCK COMPLY process?

Typical data may include company profile information, user account details, compliance records, AI system metadata, risk notes, document metadata, vendor records, owner assignments and support/demo contact information.

Customers should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive data unless the agreed plan and controls support that use case.

## 9. Where is data stored?

RISCK COMPLY is designed around managed cloud infrastructure. The exact production region, subprocessors and contractual commitments should be confirmed in the Trust Center, DPA and signed agreement.

## 10. Is data encrypted?

RISCK COMPLY is designed to rely on managed-provider encryption in transit and at rest where supported by the underlying infrastructure. Specific commitments should be verified against the Trust Center and customer contract.

## 11. Does RISCK COMPLY support role-based access?

Yes. RISCK COMPLY is designed around organization workspaces and role-based access patterns so teams can separate owners, admins and members according to their responsibilities.

## 12. Does RISCK COMPLY have audit trails?

RISCK COMPLY is designed to record important workflow activity so teams can review changes, ownership and evidence history.

## 13. Do you have SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

Do not claim completed certifications unless they are present in the live Trust Center and contract pack. If a buyer asks, answer with the current evidence available and the roadmap.

Suggested answer:

“RISCK COMPLY keeps security claims tied to documented evidence. We can share the current Trust Center materials during enterprise review.”

## 14. Do you sign a DPA?

For Business and Enterprise customers, DPA review can be part of the procurement process. Final terms depend on the signed agreement.

## 15. Do you list subprocessors?

Subprocessor information should be handled through the Trust Center or enterprise procurement pack. Keep this list updated before enterprise sales conversations.

## 16. How does onboarding work?

Typical onboarding:

1. Discovery and current AI governance workflow mapping.
2. Workspace setup.
3. Import or manual entry of first AI systems, risks, vendors and documents.
4. Owner mapping.
5. First evidence pack and dashboard review.
6. Team training.

For smaller teams, onboarding can be self-serve. For enterprise customers, assisted onboarding can be scoped commercially.

## 17. How long does onboarding take?

A focused first workspace can be ready quickly when the customer has a basic AI tools list, risk list, vendor list and document inventory. Enterprise timelines depend on data quality, number of teams, migration volume and procurement requirements.

## 20. Can RISCK COMPLY replace our spreadsheet immediately?

Yes for a first workflow, if the customer can provide a clear starting point: AI tools list, risk list, vendor list, documents and owners. Start with one use case, prove value, then expand.

# Spreadsheet / Manual Process vs Risck Comply

## Executive summary

Spreadsheets are useful to start AI compliance work, but they become fragile when the company needs ownership, review cycles, AI system evidence, audit history and executive visibility.

Risck Comply is designed to turn scattered AI governance and compliance readiness work into an accountable operating system.

## Comparison table

| Area | Spreadsheet / manual process | Risck Comply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI system ownership | Owner often stored as plain text and becomes outdated. | Owners are part of the workflow and can be reviewed with status and actions. |
| AI inventory | Systems are often listed across files, tickets or interviews. | AI systems can be registered with owner, provider, usage context and risk signals. |
| Risk classification | Risk is usually subjective and hard to compare. | Risk signals can be structured and reviewed consistently. |
| Document control | Files are spread across folders and versions. | Documents can be tracked with owner, status, review date and evidence context. |

| Audit readiness | Evidence is collected manually when requested. | Evidence can be organized continuously and reviewed before external pressure. |
| Executive reporting | Manual slide creation and spreadsheet summaries. | Dashboard view of open actions, risk posture and missing evidence. |
| Accountability | Hard to know who changed what and why. | Activity history supports review of important changes. |
| Scalability | Breaks as teams, countries, systems, vendors and documents grow. | Designed for structured growth across teams and AI governance workflows. |
| Buyer confidence | Answers depend on manual follow-up. | Answers can be tied to structured records and evidence. |

## When spreadsheets are still acceptable

A spreadsheet may be enough when:

- The company has very few AI systems.
- One person owns all readiness work.
- There are no enterprise customer reviews.
- There is no need for audit history.
- Review dates and owners rarely change.

## When Risck Comply becomes valuable

Risck Comply becomes valuable when:

- Several people own AI governance or compliance tasks.
- AI systems and vendors need review.
- Customers ask for evidence.
- Policies need review cycles.
- Leadership wants visibility.
- Audit preparation is taking too much time.

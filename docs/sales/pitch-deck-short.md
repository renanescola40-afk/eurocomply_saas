# RISCK COMPLY — Short Pitch Deck

Use this as an 8-slide first-call deck. Keep the visual style dark, premium and minimal.

## Slide 1 — Title

**RISCK COMPLY**

The European compliance control room for risks, documents, vendors and audit evidence.

## Slide 2 — Problem

Compliance work is scattered:

- Risks live in spreadsheets.
- Documents live in folders.
- Vendor evidence lives in email.
- Ownership is unclear until something is overdue.

Result: slow reviews, weak visibility and duplicated work.

## Slide 3 — Hidden cost

Manual process creates hidden cost:

- Hours lost updating spreadsheets.
- Time wasted chasing owners.
- Delayed customer reviews.
- Vendor renewals approved with missing information.
- Audit preparation starts too late.

## Slide 4 — Solution

RISCK COMPLY centralizes:

- Risk register.
- Controlled documents.
- Vendor reviews.
- Compliance actions.
- Activity history.
- Ownership and deadlines.

## Slide 5 — Product pillars

1. Risk control — severity, owners, status and mitigation.
2. Document control — review cycles, versions and accountability.
3. Vendor control — criticality, DPA status and evidence gaps.
4. Audit readiness — activity history, evidence packs and reporting.
5. Enterprise trust — precise answers for buyer review.

## Slide 6 — Buyer value

CFO:

- Lower manual coordination cost.
- Faster procurement and customer reviews.
- Better executive visibility.

Compliance Manager:

- Fewer spreadsheets.
- Clear ownership.
- Better audit preparation.

DPO:

- Better vendor visibility.
- More consistent evidence collection.
- Clearer operational accountability.

## Slide 7 — Why now

European companies need clearer control across privacy, vendors, internal policies and operational compliance.

Spreadsheets can start the process, but they do not scale ownership, evidence, workflow or visibility.

# Warranties, Indemnity and Liability Matrix

Status: DRAFT_READY / COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

| Area | Draft seller position | Typical buyer ask | Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate authority | fundamental warranty | broad authority/capacity | external corporate records |
| IP | ownership/right to transfer as disclosed | non-infringement, complete chain of title | knowledge + disclosure where appropriate |
| Security | controls/evidence accurately disclosed | no vulnerabilities/incidents | avoid absolute “secure” promise |
| Data protection | material compliance/processes as disclosed | full legal compliance warranty | counsel review; disclose known gaps |
| Financial | accounts/info fairly presented to agreed standard | GAAP/IFRS-style absolute accuracy | define records/period |
| No unreported liabilities | no liabilities outside disclosed/agreed categories | broad all-liabilities warranty | materiality/accounting qualifiers |
| Tax | filings/payments as disclosed | comprehensive historical tax warranty | tax counsel/tax covenant |
| Litigation | no known material claims except disclosed | no threatened claims | knowledge qualifier |

## Indemnity framework
Specific indemnities only for identified diligence risks. Define trigger, causation, notice, defence control, settlement consent, mitigation, tax/insurance recoveries and no double recovery.

## Liability cap options
Fundamental warranties: [50-100%] or purchase price, subject to counsel.
General warranties: [10-30%] illustrative negotiation range, not recommendation.
Specific indemnities: bespoke.
Fraud/wilful misconduct: treatment subject to mandatory law.

## Survival matrix
Authority/title: longer/fundamental period.
Tax: statutory period plus buffer where lawful.
General warranties: commonly 12-24 months, negotiated.
Data/security/IP: risk-based negotiated period.
Covenants: according to performance period.

## Materiality / knowledge qualifier matrix
Use “Seller’s Knowledge” only with named knowledge group and reasonable-enquiry standard if negotiated.
Avoid double materiality if purchase agreement already includes a materiality scrape unless intentionally negotiated.
Disclosure qualifies warranties only to the extent sufficiently specific/fairly disclosed.

## Core LOI
Transaction: proposed [SHARE / ASSET / SOFTWARE-IP / BUSINESS / HYBRID] acquisition of RISCK COMPLY.
Purchase price: [amount] [currency], subject to structure and agreed adjustments.
Consideration mix: [cash at closing] + [deferred] + [earn-out] + [escrow/holdback].
Diligence: satisfactory completion of legal, financial, tax, technical, privacy, security, IP and commercial diligence.
Definitive documents: SPA/APA and schedules, disclosure letter, TSA/KT as required.
Conditions: approvals, no material adverse change if negotiated, required third-party consents, agreed transfer mechanics, completion deliverables.
Non-binding: commercial terms are non-binding except expressly identified binding clauses.

## Exclusivity options
A — none.
B — 30-day exclusivity limited to active definitive-agreement negotiation.
C — 45-60 day exclusivity conditioned on buyer diligence milestones and automatic expiry for inactivity.
Seller preference: narrow scope, short term, clear end date, buyer milestone obligations.

## No-shop options
Narrow: no solicitation of competing written acquisition proposals during exclusivity.
Medium: no solicitation or active negotiation, but permit responses required by law.
Avoid: indefinite restrictions, restrictions after buyer inactivity, broad restrictions on ordinary commercial activity.

## Purchase price structures
Cash at closing: simplest; seller prefers cleared funds at closing.
Deferred consideration: fixed later instalments; require payment dates, security/guarantees, acceleration and no discretionary conditions.
Earn-out: define metric, accounting rules, control covenants, information rights, dispute mechanism, anti-avoidance and cap.
Escrow/holdback: specify amount, duration, permitted claims and release mechanics.
Debt-free/cash-free and working capital: use only where relevant to a share/business transfer; define debt, cash, target working capital and leakage precisely.

## Binding provisions commonly retained
Confidentiality; exclusivity/no-shop; costs; governing law; access protocol; publicity; no-shop remedies where appropriate.

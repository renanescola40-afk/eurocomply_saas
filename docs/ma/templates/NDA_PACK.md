## 1. Mutual NDA
Parties: [SELLER] and [BUYER].
Purpose: evaluate a potential acquisition of RISCK COMPLY and/or related software/IP/business assets.
Confidential Information includes product, code, architecture, security, financial, commercial, legal, customer/pipeline, pricing, transaction discussions and diligence material.
Recipient obligations: use only for Purpose; need-to-know disclosure to Representatives bound by confidentiality; reasonable protection; no reverse engineering except as expressly agreed; prompt notice of compelled disclosure; return/delete on request subject to legal backups.
Exclusions: public without breach; already known lawfully; independently developed; lawfully received from third party.
No licence/no commitment: disclosure grants no IP licence and neither party must proceed unless definitive agreements are signed.
Residuals: excluded unless expressly negotiated.
Standstill/non-solicit: not included by default.
Term: confidentiality [2-5] years; trade secrets while protected by applicable law.
Governing law/jurisdiction: [TO NEGOTIATE].
Data room: access is revocable and does not constitute a representation beyond definitive agreement.
No warranty on diligence material except as expressly stated in definitive agreement.

## 2. Seller-friendly version
Add: strict purpose limitation; no competitor benchmarking; no contact with customers/employees/suppliers without seller consent; no source-code access absent separate written approval; no AI/model training using confidential material; recipient responsible for Representatives; injunctive-relief language where enforceable; explicit transaction-existence confidentiality.

## 3. Buyer-friendly fallback
Permit broader professional advisers and financing sources; reasonable residual backup retention; narrower non-contact provisions; shorter general confidentiality period; mutual publicity restriction; compelled disclosure without prior notice only where legally prohibited.

## 4. NDA negotiation playbook
Preferred: mutual, 3-5 years, no residuals, no unrestricted source-code access, no customer contact, no disclosure of deal existence.
Acceptable fallback: 2 years for ordinary information but trade secrets survive; financing-source disclosure under equivalent confidentiality; archival copies retained only for legal/compliance.
Escalate: residuals clause, broad licence, unilateral NDA against seller, unrestricted reverse engineering, buyer right to contact customers/team, exclusivity hidden inside NDA, governing law creating major enforcement burden.
Never concede silently: IP licence, source transfer, public announcement, buyer ownership of feedback, waiver of privilege.

# RISCK COMPLY — Buyer Negotiation Playbook

Status: DRAFT_READY / COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

## Priorities
1. Maximise cash certainty and minimise contingent consideration.
2. Keep warranties evidence-bound and disclosure-qualified.
3. Avoid unlimited liability except where mandatory/strategically accepted.
4. Keep source-code/customer access staged and controlled.
5. Make exclusivity short, milestone-based and revocable for buyer inactivity.
6. Separate product diligence facts from legal conclusions.

## Economic levers
Prefer cash at closing. Deferred consideration requires fixed dates/security. Earn-out must use objective metrics, accounting consistency, buyer operating covenants and dispute resolution. Escrow should be limited in amount, claims and duration.

## Legal levers
Fundamental warranties vs general warranties; cap/basket; survival; materiality scrape; knowledge qualifiers; exclusive remedy; specific indemnities; tax covenant; governing law; dispute forum.

## Red flags
Indefinite exclusivity; uncapped general indemnity; “compliance with all laws” without qualification; absolute cyber-security warranty; buyer-controlled earn-out without covenants; transfer of unknown liabilities; unrestricted pre-closing customer contact; source-code delivery before NDA/late-stage diligence.

## Buyer counsel Q&A
Q: Is IP ownership clean?
A: Provide canonical IP/software diligence index and actual assignment/contributor evidence; do not overstate.
Q: Any security incidents or vulnerabilities?
A: Disclose factual known matters and current evidence; distinguish historical findings, remediation and external retest status.
Q: Is GDPR fully compliant?
A: Describe controls and counsel-review boundaries; no absolute compliance guarantee.
Q: Can all cloud accounts transfer?
A: Account/provider specific; some require migration/new owner account.
Q: Why asset sale vs share sale?
A: Answer based on selected perimeter, tax/corporate advice, assignability and liability allocation.
Q: Are customers/revenue guaranteed?
A: No; only provide evidenced contracts, pipeline and financial records.

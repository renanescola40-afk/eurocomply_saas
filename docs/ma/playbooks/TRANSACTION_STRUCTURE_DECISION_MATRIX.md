# Transaction Structure Decision Matrix

Status: DRAFT_READY / COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

| Structure | When to use | Seller advantages | Seller risks | Buyer advantages | Buyer risks | Tax questions | IP effect | Contract effect | Account effect | People effect | Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Share sale | buyer acquires company owning business/assets | cleaner asset continuity, fewer assignments | warranties/history transfer with company | continuity | inherits liabilities | capital gains, leakage, debt/cash | stays in company | change-of-control review | often stays, provider ToS apply | employment usually continues | Medium |
| Asset sale | selected business assets acquired | excludes unrelated assets/liabilities | many transfer steps, tax allocation | cherry-pick assets/liabilities | consent/assignment burden | VAT/stamp/tax allocation | assignment required | novation/consent often | migration/transfer | transfer rules may apply | High |
| Software/IP sale | strategic buyer wants code/IP only | narrow perimeter | title chain and migration scrutiny | focused acquisition | may lack contracts/data/people | IP transfer taxes/VAT | direct assignment | usually excluded unless listed | rebuild/migrate | usually excluded | Medium |
| Business transfer | operating business package | can monetize whole operation | labour/contract/regulatory complexity | operating continuity | inherited obligations depending law | going-concern/VAT/employment | included per perimeter | transfer regime specific | migrate/transfer | employee transfer may arise | High |
| Hybrid | combine shares/assets/IP/TSA | optimises perimeter/economics | complexity, inconsistent treatment | tailored solution | execution risk | multi-part allocation | mixed | mixed | mixed | mixed | Very high |

## Seller selection questions
1. Is the software/IP legally held by the entity whose shares would be sold?
2. Does buyer want liabilities/history or only technology?
3. Which contracts/accounts are non-transferable?
4. Is customer/personal data necessary for value and lawful to transfer?
5. Which structure creates the cleanest tax result after adviser review?
6. Does seller need post-close TSA/creator support?

# Jurisdiction, Tax and FX Checklists

Status: DRAFT_READY / COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BUYER_SPECIFIC_INPUT_REQUIRED / EXTERNAL_FACT_REQUIRED

## Portugal seller / foreign buyer
Verify seller entity legal name, CAE/business scope, articles, beneficial ownership/registry, corporate authority, tax residence, VAT/stamp/capital-gains treatment, IP ownership chain, labour implications, foreign-investment/sector rules if triggered, payment/banking documentation and Portuguese-law execution formalities.

## EU buyer
Check merger-control thresholds where relevant, FDI screening regimes, GDPR controller/processor consequences, employee transfer rules, cross-border VAT/tax, sanctions/AML onboarding, competition-sensitive diligence and data-room clean-team needs.

## US buyer
Check buyer entity and authority, CFIUS only if facts trigger US jurisdiction, export/sanctions questions where relevant, securities/financing representations if requested, Delaware/New York law implications if proposed, tax withholding/treaty analysis and US-style reps/indemnity expectations.

## Brazilian buyer
Check Brazilian buyer authority, BACEN/FX remittance mechanics, tax withholding/treaty analysis, competition/CADE thresholds if relevant, LGPD interaction for data transfers, Portuguese/Brazilian document legalization/apostille needs where applicable.

## FX/currency checklist
Deal currency [EUR/USD/BRL/other]; fixing date/rate source; who bears FX risk; escrow currency; deferred/earn-out currency; bank charges; withholding; conversion timing; tax-accounting currency.

## Tax counsel questions
Share sale vs asset/IP sale effective tax burden?
VAT/stamp duty?
Capital gains and participation-exemption possibilities?
Transfer pricing/related-party issues?
Purchase-price allocation and amortisation consequences?
Withholding on deferred/earn-out/royalty-like elements?
Treatment of escrow/holdback?
Cross-border treaty benefits and substance requirements?
Tax liabilities surviving closing?
Required tax clearances/certificates?


## Governing law / jurisdiction options
Option 1 — Portuguese law + Portuguese courts: often operationally simpler for a Portuguese seller; confirm forum, language and enforceability strategy.
Option 2 — English law + English courts/arbitration: common cross-border compromise but increases foreign-counsel cost and enforcement analysis.
Option 3 — New York/Delaware law for US strategic buyers: use only after Portuguese conflicts/enforceability and tax/corporate review.
Option 4 — International arbitration: consider confidentiality, neutral forum, seat, rules, language, interim relief and enforcement under the New York Convention.
Selection must be deal-specific; mandatory Portuguese/EU rules may apply regardless of chosen law.

# Signing & Closing Master Checklist

Status: DRAFT_READY / COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED / BUYER_SPECIFIC_INPUT_REQUIRED

## Conditions precedent
- final transaction structure approved;
- buyer/seller identities and signatories verified;
- corporate approvals obtained;
- required regulatory/third-party consents identified and obtained or waived;
- diligence completion standard agreed;
- disclosure letter/schedules final;
- tax treatment and price allocation reviewed;
- funds-flow final;
- transferability plan for code/domain/brand/cloud/contracts complete;
- data migration/privacy plan approved where personal data is involved.

## Signing
- NDA status confirmed;
- SPA/APA final;
- disclosure letter final;
- TSA/KT documents final if required;
- board/member approvals attached where applicable;
- signatory matrix complete;
- signature blocks and e-signature mechanics confirmed;
- no document labelled EXECUTED before signatures are evidenced.

## Closing deliverables
Seller: transfer instruments; approvals; agreed repositories/assets; domain/brand transfer actions; account handover/migration cooperation; corporate records for share sale; disclosure letter; invoices/tax docs where applicable.
Buyer: purchase price; escrow funding if any; buyer approvals; assumption instruments; account invitations/admin acceptance; TSA contacts.
Joint: closing memorandum, funds receipt confirmation, release of escrow instructions, post-close task list.

## Signatory matrix
Seller entity | required authority | signer | evidence | document set.
Buyer entity | required authority | signer | evidence | document set.
Creator/founder | only if personally party to KT/IP instrument | signer | evidence | scoped docs.

## Board/member approval template
“RESOLVED: the proposed [transaction] on substantially the terms presented is approved; [name/title] is authorised to finalise and execute the transaction documents and take actions reasonably necessary to complete it, subject to applicable law and constitutional documents.”
Counsel must tailor to actual Portuguese entity form and transaction.

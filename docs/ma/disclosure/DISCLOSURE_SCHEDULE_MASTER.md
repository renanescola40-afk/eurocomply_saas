1. SOFTWARE/IP TRANSFER — repos, code, docs, architecture, databases/migrations, build/release assets.

3. BRAND/TRADEMARK — names, logos, registrations/applications if any, design files and included rights.
4. SOURCE CODE — repository, branch, transfer SHA, excluded secrets, contributor history, delivery method.
5. CLOUD ACCOUNTS — Vercel, Supabase, Stripe and other providers; transferability vs migration; owner/admin handover; no passwords recorded.
6. CUSTOMER/PIPELINE — signed customers, pilots, LOIs, prospects, restrictions on disclosure; factual only.
7. SUPPLIERS/SUBPROCESSORS — canonical privacy/provider registers and material contracts.
8. OSS/SBOM — package-lock/SBOM/license evidence; known exceptions.
9. SECURITY — security architecture, pentest status, incidents/claims, known open remediation boundaries.
10. PRIVACY/GDPR — data inventory, ROPA, DPA/subprocessor/transfer positions, DSR processes.
11. TAX — registrations, returns, liabilities, advisers and open matters from authoritative records.
12. FINANCIAL — management accounts, bank/cash/debt, liabilities, recurring costs and revenue evidence.
13. LITIGATION/CLAIMS — actual, threatened or known disputes/claims from authoritative records.
14. CONTRACTS/CONSENTS — assignment/change-of-control restrictions.
15. PEOPLE — employees/contractors/contributors, roles, agreements, IP assignment status where applicable.

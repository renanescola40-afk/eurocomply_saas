This Disclosure Letter qualifies the warranties and other statements in the definitive agreement only to the extent expressly provided by that agreement.

## 1. General disclosures
Matters fairly disclosed in agreed public registers, constitutional documents and the specifically identified data-room index may qualify warranties only if the definitive agreement expressly permits general disclosure and the disclosure standard is satisfied.

## 2. Specific disclosures
For each warranty:
- warranty/clause reference;
- fact disclosed;
- supporting document/evidence;
- impact/amount if known;
- remediation/status;
- whether buyer consent/acknowledgement is requested.

## 3. Disclosure standard
No document dump. A disclosure should contain sufficient detail for a reasonable buyer to identify the nature and scope of the matter. Cross-reference canonical evidence rather than duplicating it.

## 4. No expansion
Disclosure does not create an additional representation, covenant or admission beyond the definitive agreement.

Attach the completed DISCLOSURE_SCHEDULE_MASTER and any buyer-specific schedules.

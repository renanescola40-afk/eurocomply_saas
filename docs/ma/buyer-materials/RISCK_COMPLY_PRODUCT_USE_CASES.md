# RISCK COMPLY — Product Use Cases

Date: 2026-10-07
Important: these are product use cases, not customer case studies. No named-customer deployment is implied.

| Product use case | Business problem | Product capability | Governance / regulatory context | Potential acquirer integration value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI system inventory | Organizations lack one accountable register of AI systems | Central inventory, ownership and system context | AI governance foundation and AI Act scoping | Add governance inventory to existing enterprise platforms |
| EU AI Act risk classification | Teams struggle to translate system facts into regulatory posture | Structured classification / reassessment workflows | Prohibited, limited/transparency and high-risk governance boundaries | Embed regulation-aware decision support into GRC/AI products |
| High-risk AI governance | High-risk systems require repeatable evidence and control workflows | Governance records, assessments, documentation and auditability | High-risk lifecycle governance | Accelerate regulated-enterprise feature coverage |
| Prohibited-practice screening | Organizations need early detection/escalation of prohibited uses | Screening / decision workflow where supported | EU AI Act prohibited-practice governance | Add pre-deployment policy gates |
| FRIA-related governance | Deployers may need structured fundamental-rights assessment workflows | FRIA-related workflow/documentation support where implemented | Fundamental-rights governance | Extend risk workflows for regulated deployments |
| Third-party / GPAI governance | Enterprises consume external models/providers with incomplete oversight | Vendor/model governance context and evidence | Third-party/GPAI governance boundaries where supported | Join AI vendor governance with procurement/risk products |
| Procurement due diligence | Procurement teams need evidence before approving AI vendors/systems | Procurement/trust packs, vendor governance and evidence surfaces | Third-party risk / enterprise procurement | Integrate into vendor-risk/procurement suites |
| AI governance evidence | Policies alone are insufficient without traceable evidence | Evidence capture, audit trail, exports and documentation | Auditability and internal control | Provide proof layer for enterprise AI programs |
| Documentation generation | Manual compliance documentation is repetitive and inconsistent | Structured document generation and governance artifacts | AI Act / policy documentation support | Reduce manual compliance workflow burden |
| Transparency obligations | Customer-facing or regulated AI may require disclosures and notices | Transparency workflow/document support | EU AI Act transparency duties where applicable | Add compliance outputs to communication/workflow products |
| Regulatory monitoring | Governance posture becomes stale as obligations change | Monitoring/preferences/regulatory-update workflows | Ongoing compliance lifecycle | Add regulatory-change operations to installed base |
| Multi-tenant governance | Service providers or groups need separated workspaces and roles | Organization/workspace controls, RBAC, RLS tenant boundary | Enterprise access and segregation expectations | Support partner/MSP or multi-entity use cases |
| Executive compliance reporting | Leadership needs a concise view of AI risk and evidence | Dashboard/reporting/evidence surfaces | Governance oversight | Add management-level AI risk visibility |

## Buyer diligence rule

A buyer may request demonstration of any use case. Demonstrations must use implemented flows and synthetic/dedicated demo data unless a real customer deployment is separately proven and approved for disclosure.

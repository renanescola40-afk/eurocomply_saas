## Explicit exclusions from automatic sharing

- credentials and secrets;
- production passwords or tokens;
- unnecessary personal data;
- raw confidential security findings outside controlled diligence;
- privileged legal advice;
- unrelated seller-entity assets;
- assets not proven to be owned/transferable.

## Current transaction-structure boundary

The repository does not support declaring one universally optimal sale structure. Existing diligence indicates an asset/IP-led transaction may be operationally clean only after creator-to-seller chain/title gaps are validly closed. A share sale does not automatically cure assets the seller never validly acquired.

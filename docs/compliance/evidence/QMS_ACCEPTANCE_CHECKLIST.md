# Quality Management System Acceptance Checklist

A QMS control may be promoted only when every applicable item is supported by accepted evidence from the exact integrated SHA.

- [ ] QMS scope identifies covered AI systems, operator roles and organizational boundaries.
- [ ] Quality policy is approved, versioned and effective.
- [ ] Responsibilities and authorities are assigned.
- [ ] Owner, reviewer and approver satisfy separation-of-duties requirements.
- [ ] Controlled-document lifecycle is operating.
- [ ] Record retention and integrity controls are operating.
- [ ] Design and development controls are linked.
- [ ] Supplier and third-party controls are linked.
- [ ] Data-governance controls are linked.
- [ ] Risk-management lifecycle is linked.
- [ ] Post-market monitoring is linked.
- [ ] Incident and corrective-action lifecycle is linked.
- [ ] Change-control process is operating.
- [ ] Competence and AI-literacy evidence is linked.
- [ ] Internal audit is complete and findings are recorded.
- [ ] Management review is complete and approved.
- [ ] Every severe nonconformity has containment, root cause and corrective action.
- [ ] Corrective-action effectiveness is independently verified.
- [ ] No severe nonconformity remains open.
- [ ] No corrective action is overdue.
- [ ] Regulatory and conformity strategy has accountable human review.
- [ ] Evidence references use integrity digests and bounded metadata.
- [ ] Material decisions remain append-only.
- [ ] Migration succeeds in an isolated database.
- [ ] Positive and negative two-organization RLS tests pass.
- [ ] Cross-tenant actor references are rejected.
- [ ] Server API permission, origin, rate-limit, bounded-input and audit controls pass before mutations are exposed.
- [ ] Exact-head lint, typecheck, unit tests and build pass.
- [ ] Human methodology and legal mapping review is accepted.
- [ ] Canonical scorecard promotion records limitations and exact-SHA provenance.

Passing this checklist demonstrates evidence-backed QMS workflow readiness. It does not demonstrate certification, regulator acceptance, completed conformity assessment, CE-mark authorization or guaranteed legal compliance.

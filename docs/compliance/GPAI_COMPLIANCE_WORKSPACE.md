# GPAI Compliance Workspace

## Purpose

Support evidence preparation for general-purpose AI model provider obligations, including the additional controls applicable to models with systemic risk.

## Core obligations

- technical documentation;
- downstream provider information;
- copyright policy;
- training-content summary;
- provider contact and incident process;
- security controls and model evaluation.

Systemic-risk profiles additionally require governed risk assessment, adversarial testing, serious-incident reporting and energy-efficiency reporting.

## Fail-closed boundary

An applicable obligation must be approved, owned and linked to evidence with a digest. Missing applicability analysis, evidence, accountable owner or review blocks completion. Open-source and systemic-risk classifications require qualified review.

## Truth boundary

This workspace records preparation and accountable decisions. It does not determine model classification, verify training data, certify systemic-risk controls or replace legal counsel.

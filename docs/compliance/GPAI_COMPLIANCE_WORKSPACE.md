# GPAI Compliance Workspace

The GPAI workflow records applicability, provider role, systemic-risk assessment, model documentation, training-content summary, copyright policy, downstream-provider information, evaluations, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity and energy-efficiency evidence.

Systemic-risk models receive additional fail-closed controls. Qualified review, accountable approval, material-change reassessment and critical-finding closure are mandatory before the workflow can become review-ready.

The workspace supports evidence preparation and operational governance. It does not determine legal classification, establish conformity, certify a model or replace legal, technical or domain review.

# High-Risk Provider Runtime Evidence Closure

## Objective

Close the product-runtime evidence gap for the nine-point `HIGH-RISK-PROVIDER` workstream without converting repository assertions into claims about customer data quality or legal compliance.

## Product surface covered

The evidence contract binds the exact assessed SHA to the operational provider-data workflow:

- deterministic high-risk provider data-governance logic;
- authenticated and tenant-derived API context;
- read/write RBAC;
- trusted-origin mutation guard;
- bounded Zod request parsing;
- fail-closed distributed rate limiting;
- versioned provider programmes;
- tenant-scoped dataset inventory;
- atomic approval transitions;
- audit-event persistence and compensation behavior;
- customer workspace and Regulatory Control Tower visibility.

## Evidence model

`High-Risk Provider Runtime Evidence` executes the focused unit and operational contract suites before generating a machine-readable artifact for the exact workflow SHA. The artifact includes a source digest, ten explicit control results, limitations and a SHA-256 integrity digest.

The canonical scorecard consumes the artifact through an evidence overlay. The repository does not commit a fabricated passing runtime file.

## Truth boundary

A passing artifact means the exact repository revision and CI workflow satisfy the encoded product invariants. It does **not** prove:

- correctness, completeness or representativeness of a customer's datasets;
- absence of bias;
- source licences or lawful processing;
- technical validity of a customer's AI system;
- conformity assessment, CE eligibility or regulator acceptance;
- legal compliance.

The qualified high-risk-provider methodology review remains a separate human gate and is not generated by this workflow.

## Promotion state

After a successful exact-SHA workflow run, this workstream may advance to `RUNTIME_VERIFIED`. It remains `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` until the independent qualified review is genuinely accepted.

## Evidence-based conclusion

The repository does **not** support declaring a single universally best structure today. The cleanest operational direction for a software-product carve-out appears to be an **asset/IP-led transaction only after the creator-to-seller chain and asset-title gaps are validly closed**. A share sale may reduce individual assignment mechanics for assets already owned by the company, but it does not cure assets the company never validly acquired and introduces broader company-level diligence.

This is a transaction-readiness analysis, not legal or tax advice. Final structure must reflect current corporate records, actual title, liabilities, buyer preference and professional advice.

# RISCK COMPLY — IP Chain of Title Evidence Pack

Date: 2026-10-06  
Status: `TRANSACTION_READY_DRAFT / NOT_EXECUTED`

This pack defines the asset scope and evidence required to close the creator-to-seller title chain. It does not transfer rights and must not be described as executed.

## Proposed transfer scope

Subject to professional review and applicable law, the execution instrument should expressly address:

- source code and object code;
- repository rights and repository administration/control;
- database schema, migrations, data models and database-right interests legally transferable;
- technical and product documentation;
- policies, templates and generated/document-generation materials owned by the assignor;
- UI/UX, logo, design system and original design assets;
- RISCK COMPLY brand rights and any filings/registrations actually owned by the assignor;
- domain-related contractual/control rights to the extent transferable;
- deployment/configuration materials excluding third-party secrets and non-transferable provider rights;
- derivative works and historical modifications;
- maintenance deliverables and handoff materials existing at the effective date;
- know-how specifically embodied in the transferred assets;
- warranties limited to facts the assignor can truthfully make;
- explicit carve-outs for OSS, third-party code/assets, provider terms and non-transferable rights.

## Execution evidence required before title can be marked closed

- complete legal names/identifiers of assignor and assignee;
- definitive asset schedule;
- repository and transaction cut-off SHA;
- consideration/effective date as advised;
- signature by the assignor;
- acceptance/signature by the assignee if required;
- corporate approval/authority evidence if required;
- treatment of moral rights to the extent permitted;
- disclosure of prior grants, licenses, liens, pledges, exclusivities and disputes;
- third-party/OSS carve-out schedule;
- domain/brand/design schedules where included;
- confirmation that no materially relevant contributor remains outside the chain.

## Official-source acquisition notes

For Portugal, the permanent commercial certificate can evidence current company registration and can be requested in variants covering registration, underlying documents, articles and annual accounts. The certificate is accessed by code and can be purchased with 1–4 year validity. The RCBE is the official beneficial-owner register and provides consultation/submission proof. A Portuguese tax debt/non-debt certificate is a separate AT document and is valid for 3 months. VIES validation is a point-in-time EU VAT validation and should be retained as dated closing evidence.

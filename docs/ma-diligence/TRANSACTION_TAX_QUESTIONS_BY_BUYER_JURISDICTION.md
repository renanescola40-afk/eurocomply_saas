# RISCK COMPLY — Transaction Tax Questions by Buyer Jurisdiction

Date: 2026-10-06

## Questions for every transaction

1. Is the deal a share sale, business/asset sale, or direct IP transfer?
2. Who legally owns each transferred asset at signing and closing?
3. What is the tax basis of each relevant asset/shareholding?
4. Is consideration cash, shares, earn-out, holdback or mixed?
5. Which entity receives each payment?
6. Is any portion treated as royalty, service, non-compete, employment/consulting or deferred consideration?
7. Is VAT chargeable, exempt, outside scope or subject to reverse charge?
8. Is withholding required in buyer jurisdiction?
9. Which treaty, if any, applies?
10. What FX conversion rule and date must be used?
11. Is purchase-price allocation required?
12. Which filings/certificates must be delivered at signing or closing?

## Portugal seller / EU buyer

Confirm buyer VAT status and VIES validation, exact supply/deal characterization, place-of-supply rule, reverse-charge applicability, invoice wording, recapitulative-reporting obligations if applicable, direct-tax treatment and any buyer-country withholding.

## Portugal seller / US buyer

Confirm share-vs-asset/IP form, US withholding classification, treaty eligibility, beneficial-owner documentation, source-of-income characterization, payment reporting, purchase-price allocation and any US buyer forms requested.

## Portugal seller / Brazil buyer

Confirm share-vs-asset/IP form, Brazilian withholding and remittance rules, FX registration/payment mechanics, treaty position where applicable, local deductibility/transfer-pricing issues, purchase-price allocation and Portuguese seller-side consequences.

## Closing rule

No transaction tax answer becomes FINAL until buyer identity, jurisdiction, deal perimeter, consideration structure and tax-professional review are available.

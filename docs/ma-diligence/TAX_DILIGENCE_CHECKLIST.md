## Transaction tax questions

### Asset / IP sale
1. Is the purchase price allocated among software IP, domain, trademark/brand, documentation, contracts, goodwill and other assets?
2. What Portuguese corporate tax applies to gains from each asset category?
3. Does VAT apply to any part of the transfer, and could transfer-of-a-going-concern treatment be relevant?
4. Are prior development costs capitalized or expensed, and what is the tax basis?
5. Are there stamp-duty or registration consequences?
6. Are withholding taxes triggered by royalties, licenses, transition services or deferred consideration?
7. If IP is first transferred into the seller, what tax/VAT/valuation consequences arise?

### Share sale
1. Who is selling the shares and what is their tax residence?
2. What capital-gain regime applies to the shareholder?
3. Does a participation exemption or treaty provision apply?
4. Are there latent tax liabilities that remain in the company?
5. Does the buyer require tax indemnities, escrow or special warranties?

## Cross-border buyer questions

### Buyer in Brazil
- Payment currency and FX settlement route?
- Any Brazilian withholding, CIDE, IOF or import-of-services/IP rules relevant to the exact structure?
- Treaty availability and beneficial-owner requirements?
- Need for tax residency certificates, apostille or registration?
- Who bears bank/FX fees and withholding gross-up risk?

### Buyer in United States
- Asset vs equity classification for US buyer accounting/tax?
- Any US withholding or information-reporting triggered by services, royalties or earn-outs?
- Treaty/documentation requirements (including seller tax-residence evidence)?
- Purchase-price allocation and amortization expectations?
- FX mechanics and escrow/holdback tax treatment?

### Buyer in EU/EEA
- VAT treatment and place-of-supply/transfer rules for the exact asset or service components?
- VIES status of parties where relevant?
- Reverse-charge applicability?
- Tax-residence/treaty documents?
- Transfer-of-business / going-concern treatment if applicable?

## IP-specific tax questions

1. Who legally owns the IP immediately before closing?
2. Was any IP created personally and later contributed to the company?
3. Is any assignment without consideration proposed, and what tax consequences arise?
4. Does a third-party valuation need to support an intra-group/related-party contribution?
5. Are development costs/intangibles recorded in the accounts?
6. Is any withholding triggered by a license-back, transition license or earn-out tied to IP?
7. Does the buyer require a specific jurisdiction for IP holding post-closing?

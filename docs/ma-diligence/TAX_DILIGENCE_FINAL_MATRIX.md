## Authoritative reference boundary

- EU VIES provides official VAT-number validation.
- EU VAT Directive framework generally places B2B services where the customer is established and can make the customer liable under reverse charge, subject to characterization and exceptions.
- Portugal Portal das Finanças provides a certidão de dívida e não dívida to evidence tax regularity; current official guidance states it is valid for three months.
- Portugal corporate income tax rules are authoritative only as a general legal framework; seller-specific liability still requires filings and professional review.

No seller-specific tax conclusion is asserted without seller-specific source evidence.

## Balance Sheet

Cash at bank, receivables, payables, debt, taxes payable, deferred balances, fixed assets, intangible assets and equity remain UNKNOWN until accounting/bank records are supplied. Stripe balance is separately proven at EUR 0 available / EUR 0 pending and must not be substituted for bank cash.

## Cash Flow

Opening cash, operating cash flow, investing cash flow, financing cash flow and closing cash remain UNKNOWN until bank/accounting records are reconciled.

## Normalization schedules

Maintain separate schedules for:
- founder/related-party costs;
- one-off transaction costs;
- non-recurring legal/compliance spend;
- shared seller-entity costs;
- buyer-removable costs;
- buyer-additional costs;
- standalone SaaS costs;
- carve-out costs.

- monthly P&L;
- balance sheet at cut-off;
- cash-flow statement;
- normalized OPEX;
- normalized run rate;
- buyer cost base;
- standalone cost base;
- transaction adjustments;
- reconciliation back to GL and bank.
